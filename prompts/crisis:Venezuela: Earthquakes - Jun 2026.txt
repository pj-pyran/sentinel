Crisis / situation: Venezuela: Earthquakes - Jun 2026

--- Report 1: UNICEF (2026-09-02) ---
Highlights

UNICEF continued supporting the transition from emergency response to early recovery, sustaining access to essential health, nutrition, WASH, child protection and education services for children and families affected by the earthquakes.
To date, UNICEF has reached 37,901 people with primary health care services, including 27,963 people through emergency vaccination activities; 16,661 people with safe drinking water; 9,298 children, adolescents and caregivers with mental health and psychosocial support services; and 6,872 children screened for wasting.
UNICEF supported learning continuity and school readiness by reaching 2,075 children and adolescents with educational supplies and training 568 teachers and education personnel in Education in Emergencies and psychosocial support.
UNICEF has mobilized US$25.7 million against the US$65.7 million required under the Venezuela Earthquake Response Addendum, leaving a funding gap of US$40 million. With the addendum, UNICEF’s revised 2026 HAC requirements now total US$203.3 million.

--- Report 2: ETC (2026-09-01) ---
This Situation Report provides an overview of the twin earthquakes that struck Venezuela on 24 June 2026 (magnitudes 7.2 and 7.5), and their impact on telecommunications. It outlines the activities of the WFP-led Logistics and Telecommunications Cluster (LTC)1 Telecoms Area in supporting emergency communications for the humanitarian response. The Cluster is currently not formally activated for this emergency.
Summary Points

Nine weeks after the earthquakes, the Venezuela response is transitioning from emergency life-saving operations to recovery.
The inter-agency LTC Telecoms Coordinator is supporting preparations for the phased closure of temporary operational shelter camps where inter-agency telecommunications services continue to be provided, ensuring service wind-down is aligned with camp closure timelines and humanitarian operational needs.
To ensure continuity of telecommunications coordination during the transition, the inter-agency LTC Telecoms Coordinator will formally hand over responsibilities to the WFP Country Office (CO) as LTC lead agency, before his current mission ends on 06 September.
