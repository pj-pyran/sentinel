Crisis / situation: Chad

--- Report 1: UNHCR (2026-07-30) ---
UPDATE ON SITUATION IN SUDAN
Context
The security situation in Eastern Chad remains generally stable but fragile. Following clashes in the Kulbus region of Sudan, new arrivals were reported in Koulbous and Birak (Chad), where an assessment mission identified approximately 3,000 people in need of assistance. To respond to the situation, UNHCR and its partners plan to launch an asylumseeker pre-registration operation from 27 July 2026 to strengthen identification, facilitate relocation to Marasabré, and improve humanitarian assistance planning. Despite challenges related to the rainy season, logistical and operational preparations are underway for the start of the exercise. In refugee-hosting areas, several security incidents were also reported this week, including assaults against refugee women near the Metché site, an armed robbery in the same area, and a road accident involving a humanitarian partner in Aboutengué. These incidents highlight the continued need for protection and security efforts in operational areas.

--- Report 2: UNHCR (2026-07-30) ---
Context
The security situation in Chad remains generally stable but continues to be marked by persistent risks along the Sudanese border, particularly in Wadi Fira Province. On 21 July 2026, drone-related incidents were reported near the border town of Tiné, Chad. In Indour, approximately 22 km from the border, a water point was hit, resulting in unconfirmed casualties and significant livestock losses. Later the same day, another incident was reported in Bamina, located 35 km from Tiné, where an administrative vehicle was damaged. These events occurred amid intensified fighting in Darfur near the Chadian border, heightening concerns over possible spillover effects of the conflict. However, no impact on humanitarian personnel or operations was reported.
