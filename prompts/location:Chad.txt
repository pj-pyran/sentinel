Crisis / situation: Chad

--- Report 1: OCHA (2026-06-03) ---
HIGHLIGHT


CERF: two decades of rapid response, with the fund remaining a vital pillar in the face of crises and resource shortages.


A new allocation from the Regional Humanitarian Fund (FHRAOC) prioritising a localised response, involving 40 partners, the majority of whom are national NGOs.


Measles alert: over 9,823 suspected cases, including 2,156 confirmed cases, have been reported.


As the world navigates a period of unprecedented instability, marked by an increase in climate-related crises, protracted conflicts and mass population displacement, CERF has celebrated its 20th anniversary. Officially launched in March 2006, CERF was designed to provide resources within the first 72 hours of a crisis, enabling vital aid to be deployed immediately.
Established by UN General Assembly Resolution 60/124 in December 2005 and made operational in 2006, CERF has profoundly transformed the humanitarian system’s capacity to respond to emergencies. Over the past twenty years, it has mobilised nearly $10 billion for humanitarian action in more than 110 countries, including Chad, often before any other source of funding

--- Report 2: UNHCR (2026-06-02) ---
A comparative analysis of border monitoring results between April and May 2026 highlights several changes in arrival dynamics, displacement profiles, and protection risks reported by newly arrived households in Chad. While overall trends confirm the predominance of security-related factors in population movements, the data also reveal an increased concentration of flows in certain entry areas, as well as a rise in protection risks during displacement.
