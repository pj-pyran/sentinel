--- Report 2: SPC (2026-08-10) ---
Key highlights:

Nine new laboratory-confirmed dengue cases were reported in EW32, bringing the cumulative total to 76 confirmed cases.
Weekly case counts have remained above the outbreak threshold since EW24, indicating ongoing community transmission.
A total of 12 dengue-related hospitalisations (16%) have been reported; all patients have recovered and been discharged.
No severe dengue cases or dengue-related deaths have been reported to date.
DENV-1 remains the confirmed circulating serotype.
Cases continue to be reported across multiple Area Councils on Efate, with the highest proportions in Erakor, Pango, Eratap, and Port Vila.
Response activities, including enhanced surveillance, case investigations, vector control, risk communication, and community engagement, are ongoing
