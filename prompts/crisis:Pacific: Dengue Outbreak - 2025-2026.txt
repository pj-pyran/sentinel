Crisis / situation: Pacific: Dengue Outbreak - 2025-2026

--- Report 1: Govt. Tonga (2026-04-20) ---
HIGHLIGHTS: • A total of 20 confirmed dengue fever cases reported to date, including 3 new cases. • The most affected age group 10-14years, majority among male. • Ha’apai Island continues to report the highest number of cases. • Two cases are currently hospitalized. One case transferred from ‘Eua – admitted at Vaiola Hospital, and one is admitted at Princess Fusipala Hospital, Ha’apai. • No Dengue- related death reported to date. • Laboratory testing is ongoing to confirm virus type.
