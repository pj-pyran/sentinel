Crisis / situation: Pacific: Dengue Outbreak - 2025-2026

--- Report 1: SPC (2026-09-01) ---
Key highlights:

A cumulative total of 112 confirmed dengue cases have been reported since the start of the outbreak, including 19 new cases identified between 17 and 30 August 2026 (EW33-EW34).
The outbreak remains active, with ongoing local transmission in affected communities across South-West Efate, Shefa Province.
Weekly case counts have remained above the outbreak threshold since EW24 and peaked at 15 cases in EW33.
Dengue Virus Type 1 (DENV-1) has been confirmed as the circulating serotype.
A total of 14 dengue-related hospitalisations have been reported. All patients have recovered and been discharged, and no severe dengue cases or deaths have been recorded to date.
The most affected areas continue to be Erakor (31%), Port Vila (22%), Eratap (21%), and Pango (16%).
Most cases (72%) are among individuals aged under 26 years, with the highest burden observed among children aged 5-15 years.
