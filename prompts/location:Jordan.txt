Crisis / situation: Jordan

--- Report 1: UNHCR (2026-09-29) ---
I. Introduction
The Health Information System (HIS) has been actively implemented in Za'atari camp since the end of 2012. This comprehensive report covers the reporting period from 28 March to 26 June 2026 (Weeks 14 to 26). Data is compiled on a weekly basis from all operational health facilities within the camp, guaranteeing complete health surveillance and monitoring. The reporting framework includes a total of six clinics, one emergency room, and one maternity emergency center:
• IMC Emergency Room.
• IRC Clinic.
• SAMS Clinic.
• JHASi Maternity Emergency Center.
• AMR Qatari Clinic.
• Health Appeal Saudi Clinic The baseline population figure used for calculating all health indicators and metrics throughout this reporting period is the median average of 48,958 individuals.

--- Report 2: UNHCR (2026-09-28) ---
Introduction
The Health Information System (HIS) has been actively implemented in Azraq camp since April
2014. This comprehensive report covers the first quarter of 2026, spanning from 27 December
2025 to 26 June 2026 (Weeks 1 to 26).
Data is compiled on a weekly basis from all operational health facilities within the camp,
ensuring full surveillance coverage.
Reporting entities include:
• International Medical Corps (IMC): IMC Hospital, IMC Comprehensive Health Facility,
and IMC Clinic in Village 5.
• Arab Medical Relief (AMR): Comprehensive AMR Health Facility in Village 3, AMR Clinic
in Village 5, and AMR Clinic in Village 2.
• Institute for Family Health (IFH): Reproductive health services provided in Village 5 and
the Comprehensive Health Facility.
The baseline population figure utilized for calculating all health indicators in this report is the
median average for the reporting period: 33,639 individuals.
