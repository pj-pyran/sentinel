Crisis / situation: occupied Palestinian territory

--- Report 1: Insecurity Insight (2026-07-15) ---
Data collected by Insecurity Insight shows that between 07 October 2023 and 23 June 2026, at least 3,354 incidents of violence against or obstruction of access to health care took place across the occupied Palestinian territory. 95% of all incidents were attributed to Israeli forces.

Gaza: 2,339 attacks on health care were recorded during which health facilities were damaged 448 times, 770 health workers were killed and 398 arrested. Patient access to medicine was obstructed on 303 occasions.
West Bank and East Jerusalem: 947 attacks on health care were recorded during which health facilities were damaged 30 times, 12 health workers were killed and 190 arrested. Patient access to medicine was obstructed on 585 occasions.

--- Report 2: UNRWA (2026-07-15) ---
All information updated for 1 - 7 July 2026 [1]
Days 997 - 1003 since the start of the war in the Gaza Strip
Highlights
The Gaza Strip

The latest WASH Cluster monitoring, conducted in June, indicates a continued deterioration in water security, with 84 per cent of assessed households experiencing moderate-to-high water insecurity. Access to water for domestic use declined between May and June, and drinking water was identified as the most pressing humanitarian concern, reported by 62 per cent of households.
Sanitation and hygiene conditions also remain concerning. Eighty-six per cent of households reported barriers to accessing toilets and latrines, due to lack of water, privacy concerns, and poor cleanliness, among others. Environmental health risks persist, with 52 per cent of households reporting sewage or sludge near shelters and 43 per cent reporting nearby solid waste accumulation. More than half of households reported lacking hygiene items, while health concerns remain widespread, including skin diseases or scabies (64 per cent), bedbugs (31 per cent), and lice (22 per cent).

--- Report 3: WFP (2026-07-14) ---
HIGHLIGHTS

Community safety and access to food and basic services in Gaza are under increasing strain as the expansion of areas under Israeli control causes further displacement and disruptions in humanitarian assistance. The UN Humanitarian Country Team has warned of the intensifying risks such expansion poses to civilians.
Escalating humanitarian pressures in the West Bank have displaced over 3,200 Palestinians in 2026. WFP has provided food vouchers and cash assistance to 286,228 vulnerable people affected by settler violence, military operations, displacement and ongoing socioeconomic shocks.
The entry of WFP commodities into Gaza, particularly ration boxes, was significantly reduced during the reporting period due to a non-operational cargo scanner at Ashdod Port from 16 June to 1 July, adding to the challenges created by the closure of Zikim since 24 May.
