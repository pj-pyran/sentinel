Crisis / situation: occupied Palestinian territory

--- Report 1: Insecurity Insight (2026-06-04) ---
Data collected by Insecurity Insight shows that since 08 April 2026 and 12 May 2026, violence against health care across the occupied Palestinian territory has continued. This includes:

At least 27 attacks on health care recorded during this period.
Continued exposure of health facilities, health workers, and medical transport to violence despite reported efforts to reduce hostilities.

These figures highlight the ongoing vulnerability of health care in contexts where international humanitarian law protections are not consistently upheld. Since 07 October 2023, at least 3,291 attacks on health care have been recorded across Gaza and the West Bank. The growing use of drones has emerged as a threat to health care delivery in the occupied Palestinian territory, with more than 154 recorded incidents affecting health workers, facilities, transport, and service provision. The impact on specific health services is also particularly concerning. At least 61 maternal health services have been damaged, disrupted, or otherwise affected by violence and coercive measures, limiting access to care for pregnant women, mothers, and newborns.

--- Report 2: ETC (2026-06-03) ---
The WFP-led Logistics and Telecommunications Cluster (LTC) was activated as the Emergency Telecommunications Cluster (ETC) on 31 October 2023 in response to the crisis in Gaza. This Situation Report provides a monthly update on LTC telecoms activities. All references to the LTC in this report relate specifically to the telecommunications area of the cluster.
Summary Points
• LTC Telecoms in Palestine continues to adapt its response in Gaza to sustain critical communications for humanitarian operations in a highly constrained environment, with a focus on expanding secure connectivity, maintaining power and network functionality, and advocating for the entry of fuel, spare parts, and essential technical equipment.
• On 06 May, LTC Telecoms and its lead agency, WFP, met with the Humanitarian Military Interaction Access Unit (HMI) to review the import status of ICT equipment and recent changes to approval processes required by the authorities, which WFP will formally communicate to stakeholders.
• In May, LTC Telecoms established a two-year Long-Term Agreement (LTA) with Track24, covering 07 May 2026 to 06 May 2028, to support satellite-based tracking of personnel and vehicles, and shared it with UNDSS for dissemination to UN agencies.
