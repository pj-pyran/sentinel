Crisis / situation: Guatemala

--- Report 1: UNHCR (2026-10-28) ---
CONTEXT
Guatemala, a multiethnic and multilingual nation, plays a strategic role in regional mobility. In recent years, it has become an increasingly important destination for asylums-eekers. Its geographic position places Guatemala at the centre of complex displacement dynamics, generating significant challenges and critical opportunities for national institutions and humanitarian actors.
Throughout 2026, Guatemala recorded a rise in asylum applications, particularly from nationals of Honduras, El Salvador, Venezuela, and Nicaragua, while hosting 8,663 refugees and asylum-seekers. Return movements also increased, with more than 31,780 Guatemalan returnees arriving from the United States and Mexico by June, a 47.1% increase compared to the same period in 2025. Many returnees brought valuable skills but faced significant reintegration challenges, increasing the risk of redisplacement amid violence, poverty, inequality, and limited access to basic services.
Despite these challenges, Guatemala continues to advance inclusive development, disaster preparedness, and regional solidarity, with UNHCR supporting protection space, local integration and reintegration, and technical expertise on displacement and statelessness. UNHCR maintains its presence through a national office in Guatemala City and a field presence in Esquipulas, Chiquimula.
