Crisis / situation: Bangladesh

--- Report 1: Bangladesh Red Crescent (2026-07-23) ---
Bangladesh Red Crescent Society will continue to monitor the situation closely and share updated Situation Reports as new information becomes available. This report provides the latest operational updates and upcoming activities summary as humanitarian actions on the evolving monsoon-induced flooding and landslide emergency affecting Cox’s Bazar, Chattogram, Bandarban, Rangamati, Khagrachhari, Moulvibazar, Habiganj and adjacent districts of Bangladesh.
KEY ACHIEVEMENTS BY SECTOR
● Food &amp; Cash: 23,137 HH/people reached with dry food; 2,400 HH/people with locally funded cooked food.
● WASH: 4 Mobile Water Treatment Units in Bandarban and Chattogram; 34,750+ liters of safe drinking water, 1,500 purification tablets and 5,500 jerrycans distributed
● Health: 2 mobile medical teams (1,558 people supported); 1,108 ORS; MHPSS to 579 people.
● Search, Rescue &amp; Evacuation: 22,270+ evacuated/rescued; 12 rescue boats, 110 life jackets, 135 helmets/boots, 145 pairs gloves, 5 generators deployed.
● Shelter: Cox's Bazar Population Movement Operation (PMO) assisted 494 HH in Camps 11 &amp; 12.
● Human Resources &amp; Coordination: 3,500+ volunteers/CPP mobilized network-wide (1,080 directly in field distribution); EOC active 24/7; coordination meeting held 16 July with IFRC/ICRC/PNS.
WHAT WE PLAN TO DO NEXT
● Distribute 7,500 packet; 07 days food packages in Chattogram district, Cox’s Bazar, Banderban, Rangamati, Khagrachari, Habiganj and Molvibazar.
● Distribute Multipurpose Cash for 2,570 HHs in Cox’s Bazar host community and others flood affected districts.
● Scale up rescue, WTPs, food parcels, medical teams &amp; HH items to newly at-risk districts (Sylhet, Sunamganj, Netrokona, Rangpur division).
● Mobilize 5,500 pcs Tarpaulin, Sleeping mat, Mosquito net, 1000 pcs Kitchen set and 200 pcs Family tent to the affected districts with the recommendation of Shelter Cluster.
● Mobilize additional 08 Mobile Medical team, 04 Mobile Water Treatment Plant for Health and WASH support. 200 flood-affected tubewell or water point will be disinfected.
● Volunteer mobilization for Joint Need assessment.
● BDRCS is responding based on its comprehensive flood response plan (nation-wide) to assist more than 1,50,000 people with live saving humanitarian assistance and seeking BDT 38 Cr (CHF 2.5 million)

--- Report 2: WFP (2026-07-22) ---
Key highlights
• General inflation declined slightly in June yet stayed above 9%, continuing a threemonth trend of high price pressures.
• Import-dependent food commodities (edible oils, sugar) continued to maintain elevated price in the retail markets. • Liquid fuel prices and electricity tariff remained at elevated levels.
• LPG price declined sharply in July, had jumped twice in April.
• Global fertilizer price hikes may pose serious long-term risks on domestic production, price and food security.
• The IPC analysis projected 18.1 million acute food insecure people owing to Middle East conflict induced economic pressure coupled with adverse climatic conditions.
