Crisis / situation: Bangladesh

--- Report 1: UNHCR (2026-08-11) ---
This snapshot provides an overview of how UNHCR listens and responds to Rohingya refugees’ questions, concerns, and feedback in Bangladesh between July and December 2025. It focuses on the Complaints and Feedback Mechanism (CFM), which allows refugees to share issues, seek information, and report problems through multiple safe and accessible channels, including helpdesks and a toll-free helpline. In Bangladesh, the CFM operates through the Centralized Automated, Information Processing Platform (CAIPP), which helps ensure automated referral of the issues, the provision of information provided by the service provider, supported by a FAQs messages library for reliable information, and access to needed services supporting UNHCR’s commitment to transparency, accountability to affected populations (AAP) and responsive humanitarian action.
