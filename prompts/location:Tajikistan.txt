--- Report 2: IFRC (2026-07-13) ---
Description
1. Situation Overview
On 11 July 2026, following intense rainfall, flash floods and mudflows affected the villages of Darg and Kazdon in Shamtuch Jamoat, Ayni District, Sughd Region. Mudflows descended through the Surkhi, Kashak, and Jari river channels, causing significant damage to residential houses, public infrastructure, road networks, water supply systems, agricultural land, and private property.
According to the official authorities, no fatalities or injuries have been reported. Damage and needs assessments are currently underway.
Key Impacts
· Approximate more than 92 household structures damaged (accurate number under assessment);
· Secondary School No. 48 in Kazdon village affected;
· Rural Health Centre in Darg village affected;
· Grocery shops in both villages damaged;
· Roads, pedestrian pathways, drinking water supply systems, irrigation networks, riverbank protection structures, and irrigation canals damaged;
· Eleven passenger vehicles and one KamAZ truck damaged;
· Livestock losses reported (number under assessment);
· Fruit orchards, agricultural crops, and irrigated farmland affected.

Actions taken by National Society
Summary
The Red Crescent Society of Tajikistan (RCST) deployed 15 members of the National Disaster Response Team (NDRT) to the affected area to support the emergency response. The RCST team is: • conducting rapid damage and needs assessments; • coordinating with local authorities and emergency services; • identifying priority humanitarian needs;
Actions taken by others
The Chairman of Ayni District visited the affected communities and coordinated emergency response and early recovery activities. Local authorities, emergency services, municipal utilities, and community members are undertaking debris clearance, road reopening, restoration of essential services, and rapid damage assessments.
