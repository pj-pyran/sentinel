Summary of the Situation
Between epidemiological week (EW) 1 and EW 45 of 2026 (which ended on 14 November 2026), the Region of the Americas reported 58,795 confirmed cases of measles in 17 countries and territories, representing a 4.7-fold increase compared with the same period in 2025. Guatemala (34,860), Mexico (14,087), the United States (5,412), Peru (2,985), and Canada (1,128) account for the vast majority (99.5%) of confirmed cases (Map 1). A total of 84 deaths has been reported, including 53 in Guatemala, 23 in Mexico, six in the United States of America, one in Peru and one in Bolivia (1).
During EW 43 and EW 44 of 2026, the Region of the Americas reported 527 new confirmed cases of measles in seven countries and territories (Figure 1). Of the 527 new confirmed cases reported during EW 43–44, the United States accounted for 390, followed by Guatemala 73, Peru with 48, Mexico with nine, Belize with four, Canada with two, and Bolivia with one.
The 10–13 November meeting of the Regional Commission for the Monitoring and Re-Verification of Measles, Rubella, and Congenital Rubella Syndrome Elimination (RVC) reviewed the epidemiological situation of measles across the Region. Based on the Commission’s assessment and recommendations, which were subsequently endorsed by the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO) Director, Dr. Jarbas Barbosa, endemic measles transmission was determined to have been re-established in Bolivia, Mexico, and the United States, resulting in the loss of measles elimination status in all three countries.
