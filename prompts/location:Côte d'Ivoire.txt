Crisis / situation: Côte d'Ivoire

--- Report 1: WFP (2026-11-20) ---
982 people assisted in August 2026(49% female)
USD 35,905 cash transferred
9,267 smallholder farmers supported
USD 3.4 M required in the next six months (September 2026 – February 2027)

KEY HIGHLIGHTS
• WFP signed a three-year agreement with the Ministry of National Cohesion, Solidarity and the
Fight against Poverty to strengthen national capacity for disaster preparedness and
emergency response.
• WFP supported a Government-led emergency simulation exercise to test the operationalization of Côte d’Ivoire’s National Multi-Risk Contingency Plan at decentralized level, strengthening coordination and preparedness capacities.
• WFP advanced preparations for the introduction of fresh local produce into school meals in northern regions, with partners agreeing on key arrangements for direct procurement from local farmers by schools.

--- Report 2: WFP (2026-11-20) ---
4,080 people assisted in September 2026 (52% female)
9,267 smallholder farmers supported
USD 2.2 M required in the next six months (October 2026 – March 2027)

KEY HIGHLIGHTS
• The Government increased nationally funded school feeding days from 33 to 56, reflecting stronger national investment in school meals. WFP will complement this effort in 228 schools to extend meal provision to the full 120-day school year, supporting better nutrition, education and health outcomes for children.
• Under the GCF-funded Women Adapt project, WFP launched the 2026/27 agricultural insurance campaign in the Poro Region and reviewed the status of insurance payouts for the 2025 agricultural season, supporting efforts to strengthen smallholder farmers’ resilience to climate shocks.
• WFP coordinated the review of Côte d’Ivoire’s Inter-Agency Contingency Plan, bringing humanitarian partners together to validate priority risks and strengthen preparedness and response arrangements.
