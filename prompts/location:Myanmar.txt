Crisis / situation: Myanmar

--- Report 1: KHRG (2026-11-23) ---
This Incident Report describes events occurring in Billin Township, Doo Tha Htoo (Thaton) District, in May 2026. On 12 May 2026, a Burma Army fighter jet dropped two 300-pound bombs onto Zd--- village, Noh Berh Baw village tract. One of the bombs landed in the village, killing one postpartum woman named Naw J--- and injuring five other villagers, including four-year-old Naw R--- and 17-year-old Naw V---. They were all originally from Jv--- village, K’Ter Tee village tract, Dwe Lo Township, Mu Traw (Hpapun) District, and were displaced in Zd--- village. One of the five injured villagers was in critical condition, so he was transported to Vl--- hospital in Thailand, while the others received treatment at Rk--- hospital, located in Rk--- village, Htee Baw Hkee village tract, Bilin Township, Doo Tha Htoo District, administered by the Karen Department of Health and Welfare (KDHW). Additionally, two villagers’ houses were destroyed and four were damaged by the explosion of the air strike. As a consequence, villagers faced livelihood challenges, and some of them displaced to forests in fear of further attack.[1]

--- Report 2: KHRG (2026-11-23) ---
This Interview, conducted in December 2025, describes events occurring in T’Naw Th’Ree (Tanintharyi) Township, Mergui-Tavoy District, including fighting, shelling, and air strikes, which resulted in displacement, travel restrictions, healthcare challenges, difficulties accessing education, and livelihood challenges for local villagers who displaced in Za--- village, Moo K’Hpoh area. In particular, the interviewee, Ma T---, discusses livelihood challenges through the lens of gender and the specific challenges women face in the displacement site. The interview concludes with needs relating to humanitarian support, Ma T---’s hopes for the future, and requests to relevant stakeholders. This interview was conducted as part of a series of interviews examining the experiences in women in displacement in Karen State from January 2024 to December 2025. 1
