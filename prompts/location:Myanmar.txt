Crisis / situation: Myanmar

--- Report 1: Insecurity Insight (2026-10-09) ---
Data collected by Insecurity Insight shows that since the military coup on 01 February 2021 and 31 August 2026, at least 1,973 incidents of violence against or obstruction of access to health care took place across Myanmar:

health facilities were damaged 519 times
175 health workers were killed, 940 arrested and 55 kidnapped

Since January 2025, incidents involving damage to or destruction of temporary or makeshift health facilities in Myanmar have increased sharply, from four incidents between February 2021 and December 2024 to 33 between January 2025 and August 2026. In total, 89% of the reported incidents were attributed to Myanmar Armed Forces (MAF) aircraft and drone strikes. Temporary or makeshift health facilities in Sagaing Region were particularly affected, with incidents also frequently reported in Kachin and Mandalay.

--- Report 2: KHRG (2026-10-09) ---
This Incident Report describes events occurring in Waw Ray (Win Yay) Township, Dooplaya District, in June 2026. On 21 June 2026, Burma Army soldiers based in Ac--- army camp, Ac--- village, Noh Shoo Neh village tract, Waw Ray Township, fired two mortar shells, and one of the shells landed next to a villager’s house, injuring three villagers and damaging the house and some plantations in Aa--- village, Win Hk’Na village tract, Waw Ray Township. Due to this shelling, some students were afraid to go to school, and the villagers had to work in fear. Some of the students also suffered from malaria and could not go to school.[1]
