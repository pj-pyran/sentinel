--- Report 2: KHRG (2026-10-05) ---
This Incident Report describes events occurring in Noh T’Kaw (Kyainseikgyi) Township, Dooplaya District, in May 2026. On 24 May 2026, a Burma Army fighter jet dropped two 500-pound bombs in Aa--- village, Noh Tar Ghaw village tract, Noh T’Kaw Township. As a result, three women were killed, two villagers were injured, including a 16-year-old girl, and three houses, one restaurant, and one car were damaged. After the air strike, the villagers were panicked and fled temporarily to a place where they felt safer. The villagers were also worried about opening the village tract school, and some parents sent their children to schools in Thailand.
