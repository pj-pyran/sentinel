Crisis / situation: South Sudan: Floods - Jul 2025

--- Report 1: WFP (2026-09-04) ---
In July, WFP assisted 2.36 million people with 15,981 mt of food and USD 13.49 million through cash-based transfers. Of the reached, were 1.8 million crisis-affected people and 338,602 school children.
WFP will experience pipeline breaks for 400,000 refugees and IDPs after September distributions; WFP, UNHCR and IOM are engaging with the Government of South Sudan on joint actions to inform affected refugees and IDPs of the expected impact on assistance.
WFP achieved strong Targeted Supplementary Feeding Programme (TSFP) results in the first half of 2026, distributing 6,000 mt of specialized nutritious foods, representing 91 percent of target. The programme admitted 222,000 pregnant and breastfeeding women and girls and 341,000 children aged 6–59 months for treatment of moderate acute malnutrition

--- Report 2: OCHA (2026-09-02) ---
South Sudan Humanitarian Update (as of 31 August 2026)
HIGHLIGHTS

Acute nutrition needs increase across South Sudan.
Critical life-saving nutrition supplies reportedly exhausted in Upper Nile State.
Safe water access remains a critical challenge amid insecurity, drought and funding gaps.
Below-average rainfall, prolonged dry spells and above normal temperatures reported across the country.
More than 296,000 children reached with education, but major gaps remain.
The Humanitarian Coordinator visits Central Equatoria State to assess Ebola preparedness measures.
