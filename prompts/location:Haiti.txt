Crisis / situation: Haiti

--- Report 1: WFP (2026-09-07) ---
597,304 people assisted in July 2026
1,185 mt food distributed
USD 7.9 M cash transferred
USD 92 M required in the next six months (July – December 2026)

KEY HIGHLIGHTS
• The final court-ordered extension of Temporary Protected Status for Haiti expired on 27 July, creating legal uncertainty for up to 350,000 Haitian nationals residing in the US and responsible for critical remittances supporting vulnerable households. WFP continues to monitor potential return movements and related preparedness requirements.
• With preparations underway for the 2026/27 school year, as many as 280,000 children (one third of children targeted) are at risk of not receiving school meals as a result of gaps in available resources.
• Elections will take place on 13 December, subject to funding and security. Political party registration began in July, noting challenges in the Ouest – the department most affected by violence, and the most populous.

--- Report 2: WFP (2026-09-07) ---
720,409 people assisted in June 2026
1,118 mt food distributed
USD 5.3 M cash transferred
USD 74.2 M required in the next six months (July – December 2026)

KEY HIGHLIGHTS
• With the 2026 Atlantic hurricane season underway, WFP is strengthening national preparedness efforts in collaboration with the Directorate of Civil Protection through coordination support, prepositioning of contingency stocks, the establishment of anticipatory action mechanisms and early warning initiatives. Facing stressed commodity pipelines, WFP began to constitute locally sourced contingency food stocks sufficient to support up to 75,000 people during the immediate aftermath of a storm.
• Haiti is progressing with the implementation of the UN80 Integrated Supply Chain pilot initiative. In June, UN agencies finalized governance arrangements, validated a Single-Entry Point approach and confirmed operational readiness for priority logistics services.
