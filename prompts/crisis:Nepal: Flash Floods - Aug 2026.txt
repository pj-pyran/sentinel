Crisis / situation: Nepal: Flash Floods - Aug 2026

--- Report 1: OCHA (2026-09-24) ---
HIGHLIGHTS

The floods have claimed 1,453 lives; 5,644 people remain missing and 13,795 have been rescued (NDRRMA, 23 September, 19:00)
1,112 people remain in 21 displacement centres.
World Food Programme recently completed a Rapid Market Functionality and Price Monitoring Assessment, which found that while many local markets have reopened, supply chains remain under strain. Traders continue to face difficulties replenishing stocks and prolonged transport disruptions could further constrain their ability to meet demand.

--- Report 2: WFP (2026-09-24) ---
KEY HIGHLIGHTS
• The Logistics and Telecommunications Cluster facilitated the receipt, storage and onward transport of 188.9 mt of humanitarian cargo to flood-affected areas, including 65.5 mt delivered through helicopter operations.
• The cluster continued to expand common logistics services, increasing storage capacity and enabling partners to sustain humanitarian response operations across affected areas.
