Crisis / situation: Nepal: Flash Floods - Aug 2026

--- Report 1: WFP (2026-11-13) ---
SITUATION OVERVIEW
• The 26 August floods caused widespread damage across Rasuwa and downstream districts, affecting homes, livelihoods and critical infrastructure. The Government’s assessment estimates US$2.7 billion in disaster effects and US$4.77 billion in recovery and reconstruction needs.
• The UN and humanitarian partners launched a US$49.6 million Flash Appeal to support 84,270 flood-affected people from 26 August to 31 December 2026, complementing the Government-led response and recovery efforts. Food Security faces a US$1.76 million funding gap, while Logistics and Emergency Telecommunications requires US$5.12 million.
• On 11 October, further flash flooding along the Budhi Gandaki River affected Gorkha and Dhading, compounding recovery needs following the August floods.

--- Report 2: UNFPA (2026-11-11) ---
The floods that struck Nepal in late August 2026 caused extensive infrastructure damage, disrupted road connectivity and forced nearly 33,000 people to seek refuge in temporary shelters and holding centres. Further flooding along the Budhi Gandaki River on 11 October affected communities in Dhading and Gorkha, exacerbating existing vulnerabilities.
During the reporting period, three UNFPA-supported mobile teams delivered essential health services across 22 locations, reaching 2,155 people with sexual and reproductive healthcare, including 315 pregnant, breastfeeding and postpartum women. A further 3,850 people received gender-based violence prevention and response services. Additionally, 2,399 women and adolescent girls were supplied with dignity kits and 47 received cash and voucher assistance.
UNFPA requires US$3.5 million to implement its six-month flood response in Nepal. As of November 2026, US$1.86 million remains unfunded.
