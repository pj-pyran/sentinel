Crisis / situation: Yemen: Cholera Outbreak - Mar 2024

--- Report 1: Health Cluster (2026-07-19) ---
HIGHLIGHTS
A total of 846 Health Facilities (85 Hospitals, 294Health Centers , 453 Health Units, and 14 Others ) are being supported by Health Cluster Partners. - During the reporting period of January and February 2026, Health Cluster conducted a total of 9 Health Cluster coordination meetings and 19 ad hoc meetings at the National, AoR, and Hub levels (Aden, Al Hudaydah, Ibb/Taizz, Sa’dah, Sana’a, Marib, Hadramout, and West Coast). - Health Cluster conducted and attended over 40 technical meetings, which include but are not limited to ICCM, RCT, TWGs, inter-sectoral, and government meetings. The cluster also conducted 5 field visits and over 46 bilateral meetings with partners, donors, government entities, and stakeholders during the reporting period. Furthermore, the Health Cluster attended/ Conducted 2 trainings and 2 Workshops.
