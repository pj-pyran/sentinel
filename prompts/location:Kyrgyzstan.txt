--- Report 1: WFP (2026-11-10) ---
45,021 people assisted in Oct 2026 (59% female)

USD 133,100 distributed in Oct 2026

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

A visit by WFP Acting Executive Director to the Kyrgyz Republic advanced joint action on food security, climate resilience and nationally led programming.
WFP supported the Pre-COP31 National Dialogue on climate and biodiversity priorities. At COP17, WFP and the Ministry of Natural Resources highlighted local food systems and Home-Grown School Meals.
At the Central Asian Heads of Emergency Authorities’ Meeting, WFP showcased climate risk insurance, anticipatory action and pre-positioned kits for regional expansion.
Individual Food Kits were officially launched by the Ministry of Emergency Situations, with support from Switzerland and WFP, to strengthen its disaster preparedness.
Through the Government’s Inclusive Assistant Programme, WFP complemented government payments with nutritious food for over 16,000 households.
