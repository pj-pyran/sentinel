Crisis / situation: Kyrgyzstan

--- Report 1: WFP (2026-07-27) ---
Market context and drivers
• Higher energy, fertilizer, transport and food costs are increasing pressure on poorer households. Monitoring fertilizer availability and transportation costs remains critical. (World Bank, 2026)
• The government introduced temporary subsidies on fuel imports to ensure availability and stabilize retail prices. (Cabinet of Ministers, 2026)
• The Government also temporarily restricted coal exports by road to help protect domestic supplies ahead of the heating season. (Cabinet of Ministers, 2026)
• From July 2026, the new “Bala Yrysy” programme will be provided as a monthly support to families with children under three years old. (Cabinet of Ministers, 2026)
Risks
• Despite strong economic growth, high inflation, elevated food prices, declining remittances, and climate-related risks continue to pressure household purchasing power and food security, especially among vulnerable households.
• El Niño may bring more rain to Central Asia in second half of 2026. In the Kyrgyz Republic, this could increase the risk of floods, mudflows, soil erosion and damage to roads and farms.
• Fuel prices and supplies remain a concern. If regional supplies fall or wholesale prices rise, fuel and transport costs may increase despite government support.

--- Report 2: IFRC (2026-07-27) ---
Description of the Event
Date of event
13-07-2026
What happened, where and when?
On 13 July 2026, at approximately 19:30 local time, intense short-duration rainfall over the mountainous catchment areas of Nooken district triggered mudflows along two separate channels. The Besh-Korgon channel overflowed and inundated the lower streets of Kochkor-Ata city, destroying 3 electricity poles in the Besh-Korgon settlement. Simultaneously, the Kara-Bulak channel overflowed into Ergesh Aliev village, while separate mudflows descending from higher elevations submerged the villages of Kurulush, Boston, and Kop- Tam in the Mombekov village area. The event occurred in Nooken district, Jalal-Abad oblast, Kyrgyzstan, affecting Kochkor-Ata city and the villages of Ergesh Aliev, Kurulush, Boston, and Kop-Tam.
The mudflows struck the evening of 13 July 2026 (19:30 local time). District civil protection authorities were mobilized the same evening, and clearance and mud-pumping operations were still ongoing as of 02:00 on 14 July 2026. A state of emergency has been declared in the district, and the situation is being monitored by RCSK in coordination with local civil protection authorities.
