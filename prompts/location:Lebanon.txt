--- Report 5: UNFPA (2026-11-17) ---
The humanitarian situation in Lebanon remains fragile. Continued hostilities, movement restrictions and explosive ordnance contamination in southern Lebanon are limiting access to essential services and hindering safe returns. As of 28 October, 304,520 people remained internally displaced, 53 per cent of whom are women and girls.
Damage to health facilities, road closures and movement restrictions continue to limit access to care in parts of southern Lebanon. Three hospitals and 21 primary healthcare centres remain closed, while 17 hospitals have sustained damage.
Since the onset of the crisis on 2 March 2026, UNFPA has reached over 57,000 women and girls with sexual and reproductive health services and more than 56,500 people with gender-based violence prevention and response activities. UNFPA has supported 5,423 women to give birth safely in health facilities, and distributed dignity kits to nearly 37,350 women and adolescent girls.
