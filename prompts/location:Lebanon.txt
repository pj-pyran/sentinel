Crisis / situation: Lebanon

--- Report 1: AMEL (2026-07-29) ---
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
SITUATION IN LEBANON
Three months into the renewed conflict in Lebanon, nearly 1.3 million people remain displaced and the humanitarian situation continues to deteriorate, with critical infrastructure damage restricting access to food, healthcare, and assistance across South Lebanon. Amel Association International's emergency response reached 148,107 people across 207 sites in all eight governorates between March and May 2026, delivering 6,267 activities in health, protection, education, and distribution. Health teams provided 85,139 services through 15 active Primary Health Care Centres and 11 Mobile Medical Units, while protection programming reached 23,941 people through integrated General Protection, GBV, Child Protection, and MHPSS support. Education activities reached 27,282 children, and 15,091 relief items were distributed to the most vulnerable, including older people and persons with disabilities. Eight Amel centres sustained damage during the reporting period, underscoring the operational risks faced while sustaining life-saving services amid a critically underfunded Lebanon Flash Appeal.

--- Report 2: AMEL (2026-07-29) ---
Nearly four months into the renewed conflict in Lebanon, at least 4,247 people have been killed and 12,195 injured since hostilities resumed on 2 March 2026, though displacement figures show early signs of stabilization, with over 523,000 people beginning to return despite continued insecurity and destruction. Amel Association International scaled up its response to reach 194,266 people across 218 sites between March and June 2026, delivering 8,482 activities. Health services expanded to 120,455 consultations and interventions through 18 active health centres and 11 Mobile Medical Units, with Primary Support Units redeployed to match population returns. Protection programming reached 27,035 people, and education activities reached 34,077 children. Amel distributed 18,604 relief items and mourns the loss of two staff members killed in the line of duty. With the Flash Appeal only 32% funded and pipeline breaks expected from July, sustained donor support remains critical to maintaining life-saving operations.

--- Report 3: UNHCR (2026-07-29) ---
This Flash Update reports that the humanitarian situation in Lebanon remains critical despite continued returns, with significant humanitarian and protection needs persisting among displaced populations, returnees, and host communities. Ongoing hostilities in or near the IDF-declared military area continue to expose civilians to significant protection risks.
As of 23 July, the Ministry of Public Health reports at least 4,329 people were killed and 12,233 were injured. Moreover, 753,024 people reported returning to their areas of origin, while 375,090 people remain displaced, including 29,500 displaced inside 271 collective shelters. The Flash Appeal stands at 44.8 per cent funded, making it challenging to sustain humanitarian assistance.
Humanitarian partners continue to prioritize the delivery of a coordinated, multi-sector response across Lebanon while prioritizing de-escalation and cessation of hostilities, as well as the protection of civilians.
