Crisis / situation: Colombia: Earthquake - Aug 2026

--- Report 1: ETC (2026-09-01) ---
This Situation Report provides an overview of the magnitude 7.4 earthquake that struck Colombia on 10 August 2026 and its impact on telecommunications. It outlines the activities of the WFP-led Logistics and Telecommunications Cluster (LTC) 1 Telecoms Area in supporting emergency communications for the humanitarian response. The Cluster is not formally activated for this emergency but is providing coordination and technical support to humanitarian partners as required.
Summary Points
• LTC Telecoms continued engagement with government authorities and humanitarian partners to assess telecommunications needs, identify connectivity gaps and determine priority support requirements for the earthquake response.
• On 25 August, humanitarian agencies agreed to reactivate the UN ICT Working Group and identified priority requirements for satellite connectivity, resilient power solutions, radio communications, ICT equipment and technical support.
• On 29 August, an Ericsson Response telecommunications specialist deployed to Colombia to assess infrastructure damage, identify connectivity gaps and support planning for potential inter-agency telecommunications services.

--- Report 2: IOM (2026-08-30) ---
SITUATION OVERVIEW
On 10 August 2026, at 7:34 a.m., a magnitude 7.4 earthquake struck, with its epicenter in San José del Palmar, Chocó Department. Given the scale of the emergency, the National Government declared a national disaster and an Economic, Social and Ecological Emergency. Since then, Chocó, Valle del Cauca, Risaralda and Caldas have remained priority departments due to the extent of the impact.
In Colombia, emergency response is led by the National Government, within the framework of the National System for Disaster Risk Management (SNGRD) and under the coordination of the National Unit for Disaster Risk Management (UNGRD). At the territorial level, departmental and municipal authorities, together with UNGRD local staff, guide the identification of needs and response priorities.
The international humanitarian community complements the Government-led response by aligning its capacities with the priorities identified by the authorities. Coordination takes place at the national level through the Humanitarian Country Team (EHP) and, at the departmental level, through the Local Coordination Teams (ELCs), in coordination with territorial authorities.
