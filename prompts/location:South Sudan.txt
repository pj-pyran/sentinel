--- Report 1: Nutrition Cluster (2026-07-02) ---
Last Reporting Month: March 2026
24 Partners Reporting
836 Functional Nutrition Sites
NUTRITION RESPONSE SUMMARY
2025 Annual Achievements &amp; Q1 2026 Update
The South Sudan Nutrition Cluster sustained life-saving services throughout 2025 in an operating environment characterised by escalating conflict, shrinking humanitarian access, and a progressively deteriorating funding landscape. Despite these constraints, partner organisations, comprising 24 international and national NGOs, maintained therapeutic nutrition services across 836 functional nutrition sites nationwide, achieving substantial coverage against a growing caseload that continues to outpace available resources.

--- Report 2: Concern (2026-06-30) ---
The Concern Worldwide (CWW)Accountability to Affected Populations project, supported by WFP, aims to create a bottom-up feedback and communication mechanism between responders and people we serve, that is community-based, easily accessible, and built on mutual understanding and genuine information-sharing. Through local responders and community correspondents, Concern, Community Engagement Network (CEN), and the Radio Community (TRC) work together to delivera complete community feedback mechanism that is accessible to all participants regardless of their literacy levels and access to assets and internet. Using TRC's and CEN’s longstanding experience in radio broadcasting, the project ensures a wide outreach beyond people with access to written updates. Together, and alongside WFP, the project partners respond to information and feedback gaps. By consolidating and sharing the feedback received, the project increases accountability to affected populations in the targeted areas.
