Crisis / situation: South Sudan

--- Report 1: Logistics Cluster (2026-08-14) ---
Background
South Sudan continues to face a complex humanitarian crisis driven by conflict, climate-related shocks, disease outbreaks, displacement, food insecurity and economic deterioration. Access to affected communities remains constrained by poor road conditions, seasonal flooding, and limited transport infrastructure. In this context, the WFP-led Logistics Cluster continues to enable humanitarian operations through logistics coordination, information management, capacity strengthening and common transport services, including air, river and road transport to hard-to-reach and priority locations.
Operational Highlights
• During the reporting period, the Logistics Cluster facilitated the transportation of 3,077 MT of humanitarian cargo, supporting 43 organizations and reaching 37 destinations across South Sudan.
• Road transport accounted for 46 percent of the cargo moved, followed by river transport at 39 percent and air transport at 15 percent.
• The Cluster coordinated eight humanitarian road convoys delivered approximately 1,417 MT of multisectoral supplies.
• The Logistics cluster completed 31 service requests, enabling partners to deliver critical assistance to priority and hard-to-reach locations.
• The Logistics Cluster supported the Unity State cholera response in Leer, Mayendit and Mayom by facilitating the transport of 30.2 MT of emergency relief cargo.
