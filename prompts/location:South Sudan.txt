KEY MESSAGES
The average monthly foodbasketcost remained stable at SSP 133,602 per person. --- Report 2: WHO (2026-10-12) ---
Mental health is an essential component of health and well-being, influencing how people think, feel, and function throughout their lives. People affected by conflict and displacement, survivors of gender-based violence, people living with chronic disease such as HIV/TB, persons with epilepsy and other neurological conditions, people with substance use disorders, and people facing food insecurity and chronic poverty are some of the groups at a higher risk of developing mental health disease.
Mental health conditions contribute to disability, reduced productivity, poor physical health outcomes, and increased demand for health and social services. Drawing on emerging findings from the South Sudan Annual Health Sector Performance Report 2024/25 and the 2025 Health Statistical Abstract, this knowledge product examines the status of mental health services in South Sudan and the implications of limited service availability, workforce capacity, and utilization for health system performance and universal health coverage.
