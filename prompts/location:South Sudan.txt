Crisis / situation: South Sudan

--- Report 1: WFP (2026-11-27) ---
Assistance and access: WFP reached about half a million people, distributed 2,343 metric tonnes of food and transferred USD 1.58 million in cash. Insecurity delayed deliveries in parts of Upper Nile and Jonglei, and reduced air transport capacity from November threatens deliveries to remote communities.
Famine prevention: WFP had funding from November for only three of six priority counties: Ulang, Nasir and Nyirol. Uror, Fangak and Akobo still lacked funding for the response. WFP reported an overall funding shortfall of USD 388 million for October 2026–March 2027.
Acute malnutrition: October surveys recorded rates of 28.3% in Fangak and 22.8% in Leer, both above the 15% emergency threshold. In Akobo, WFP worked with UNICEF and IRC to keep 14 supplementary feeding sites operating through December.

--- Report 2: Protection Cluster (2026-11-25) ---
In October and November 2026, on behalf of the South Sudan Protection Cluster and with support from UNHCR and ECHO, REACH Initiative used Protection Risk Monitoring (PRMS) tools to conduct 45 Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) with community members in three counties: Luakpiny/Nasir (Upper Nile), Akobo (Jonglei) and Abiemnhom (Unity) and supplemented the analysis with Key Informant Interviews (KIIs) conducted in the same areas in the preceding six months. Discussions covered the protection risks communities are experiencing amidst heightened conflict between the South Sudan People’s Defense Force (SSPDF) and the Sudan People’s Liberation Movement-In Opposition (SPLM-IO) and allied groups, the capacities they already draw on to cope, and what they say they need most.
