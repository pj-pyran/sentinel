Crisis / situation: South Sudan

--- Report 1: IOM (2026-08-19) ---
South Sudan is currently facing a severe humanitarian crisis characterised by extensive internal displacement. The underlying causes of these displacement dynamics are varied and include communal clashes, land disputes, insecurity, violence, and disasters. The situation is further complicated by the ongoing conflict in neighbouring Sudan, which has led to increased cross-border movements, and an influx of refugees and returnees, straining already limited resources.

--- Report 2: OCHA (2026-08-17) ---
HIGHLIGHTS

Humanitarian response expands in Jonglei State after local authorities provide access assurances.
An Inter-Cluster Coordination Group mission to Akobo West Payam finds more than 35,000 conflict-affected people in critical need of humanitarian assistance.
Critical funding shortfalls threaten life-saving food, nutrition and other assistance for more than 650,000 refugees and asylum seekers in South Sudan.
The Humanitarian Coordinator visits Yambio town to assess Ebola preparedness measures, health services and the humanitarian response.
At least 60 people killed and dozens injured during intercommunal violence in Tonj North County, Warrap State, on 9 August.
Humanitarian partners begin preparations for the 2027 Humanitarian Programme Cycle.
