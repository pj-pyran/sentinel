Crisis / situation: South Sudan

--- Report 1: WFP (2026-08-04) ---
WFP provided food assistance to 1.94 million people through 9,688 mt of food and USD 17.3 million in cash-based transfers across diverse programmes. The number of people reached represents 69 percent of the target for the month.
As of 30 June, WFP had assisted 1 million people through the lean season response, representing 63 percent of 1.6 million people targeted.
The lean season response in Akobo East reached three distinct beneficiary groups: 49,000 people received full general food assistance rations, 57,000 received fortified biscuits, and 21,000 received specialized nutrition assistance.

--- Report 2: Concern (2026-08-03) ---
The Concern Worldwide Accountability to Affected Populations project, supported by WFP, aims at creating a bottom-up feedback and communication mechanism between responders and people we serve, that is community-based, easily accessible, and built on mutual understanding and genuine information-sharing.
Through local responders and community correspondents, Concern Worldwide, Community Engagement Network (hereafter: CEN), and the Radio Community (hereafter: TRC) work together to set up a complete community feedback mechanism that is accessible to all participants regardless of their literacy levels and access to assets and internet. Using TRCs and CEN’s longstanding experience in radio broadcasting, the project ensures a wide outreach beyond people with access to written updates.
Together, and alongside WFP, the project partners respond to information and feedback gaps. This way, and by consolidating and sharing the feedback received, the project increases accountability to affected populations in the targeted areas.
