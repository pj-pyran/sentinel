Crisis / situation: South Sudan

--- Report 1: Protection Cluster (2026-11-25) ---
In October and November 2026, on behalf of the South Sudan Protection Cluster and with support from UNHCR and ECHO, REACH Initiative used Protection Risk Monitoring (PRMS) tools to conduct 45 Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) with community members in three counties: Luakpiny/Nasir (Upper Nile), Akobo (Jonglei) and Abiemnhom (Unity) and supplemented the analysis with Key Informant Interviews (KIIs) conducted in the same areas in the preceding six months. Discussions covered the protection risks communities are experiencing amidst heightened conflict between the South Sudan People’s Defense Force (SSPDF) and the Sudan People’s Liberation Movement-In Opposition (SPLM-IO) and allied groups, the capacities they already draw on to cope, and what they say they need most.

--- Report 2: Concern (2026-11-23) ---
The Concern Worldwide Accountability to Affected Populations project, supported by WFP, aims at creating a bottom-up feedback and communication mechanism between responders and people we serve, that is community-based, easily accessible, and built on mutual understanding and genuine information-sharing.
Through local responders and community correspondents, Concern Worldwide, Community Engagement Network (hereafter: CEN), and the Radio Community (hereafter: TRC) work together to set up a complete community feedback mechanism that is accessible to all participants regardless of their literacy levels and access to assets and internet. Drawing on TRC’s and CEN’s extensive experience in radio broadcasting and community engagement, the project ensures a wide outreach beyond people with access to written updates.
Together, and alongside WFP, the project partners respond to information and feedback gaps. This way, and by consolidating and sharing the feedback received, the project increases accountability to affected populations in the targeted areas.
