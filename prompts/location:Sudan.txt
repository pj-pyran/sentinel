Crisis / situation: Sudan

--- Report 1: WFP (2026-11-19) ---
In October, WFP assisted 3.4 million people, including 795,000 people in areas at Risk of Famine (RoF).


WFP distributed 17,000 mt of food and provided USD 11.1 million in cash-based transfers (CBT).


WFP reached 1.4 million people with GFA, 280,000 children and pregnant and breastfeeding women and girls (PBWGs) with nutrition support, 200,000 students with school feeding activities and 18,000 people through resilience and livelihoods activities.


In October, WFP made significant gains; in reaching previously difficult-to-access areas, providing in-kind food assistance to 170,000 people in East Darfur and 48,000 in West Kordofan.


While funding constraints remain the primary obstacle for implementation of WFP operations, administrative approvals, and logistics constraints continued to impede operations.


Fuel shortages and power disruptions also affected transport, silo operations, agricultural activities and programme delivery this month.
