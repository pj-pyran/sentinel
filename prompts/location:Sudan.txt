Crisis / situation: Sudan

--- Report 1: UNICEF (2026-11-24) ---
Highlights

In September, over 29,000 people were newly displaced, while more than 8.5 million remain internally displaced, placing immense pressure on basic services and humanitarian assistance.
UNICEF enabled 333,500 women and children to access primary healthcare, reached 144,200 people through emergency health services, and supported routine immunization, including 1.2 million children vaccinated with DTP1 and 919,000 with measles vaccine.
Nutrition interventions expanded, with 139,000 caregivers receiving IYCF counselling and 41,000 cartons of RUTF dispatched for severe acute malnutrition treatment.
UNICEF reached 7.7 million people with safe water services, 1.4 million children with education opportunities, and 184,784 children with psychosocial support.
As of 30 September 2026, only 40 per cent (US$382.5 million) of requirements had been secured, leaving a US$580.4 million funding gap, with critical shortfalls persisting in Child Protection, Nutrition, Health and humanitarian supply pipelines.

--- Report 2: UNHCR (2026-11-23) ---
The conflict in Sudan has created a complex displacement and protection crisis. Although over 5.2 million people have returned to areas of origin, there are still an estimated 8.6 million people internally displaced.
Over 900,000 refugees and asylum-seekers mainly from South Sudan, Ethiopia and Eritrea reside in Sudan, with few solution options available to them. This multifaceted context is increasing pressure on public service delivery.
UNHCR and partners deliver life-saving assistance and protection through community- based structures and an area-based approach that enables self-reliance.
