Crisis / situation: Sudan

--- Report 1: Insecurity Insight (2026-07-14) ---
Data from Insecurity Insight shows that since fighting started between the SAF and RSF in April 2023 and 09 June 2026 at least 768 incidents of violence against or obstruction of access to health care took place across Sudan:

Over two-thirds of these 768 attacks were attributed to the RSF
213 health workers were killed, 15 kidnapped and 129 arrested
Health facilities were damaged 218 times

During the reporting period, 42 incidents affecting health care were reported in Nyala in South Darfur where a cholera outbreak was declared on 23 June 2026, following a period of ongoing insecurity. Health facilities were damaged on 13 occasions and four health workers killed and 30 were arrested. The cumulative impact of these incidents likely weakened the capacity of the local health system before the outbreak, reducing the availability of health infrastructure and personnel needed for clinical care, disease surveillance, and infection prevention and control. Protecting health facilities, health workers and patients is essential to ensuring an effective cholera response and safeguarding civilians' access to health care.

--- Report 2: OCHA (2026-07-12) ---
HIGHLIGHTS

Violence continues to escalate in North Kordofan, taking a heavy toll on civilians and causing extensive damage to critical civilian infrastructure.
Explosive ordnance, including unexploded bombs, artillery shells, rockets, and landmines continues to pose a serious threat to families who returned to their homes.
The situation for children in Sudan remains among the most severe globally, with millions affected by ongoing violence, disrupted essential services and escalating humanitarian needs.
