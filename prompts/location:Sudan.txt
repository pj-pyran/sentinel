Crisis / situation: Sudan

--- Report 1: Insecurity Insight (2026-11-10) ---
Data by Insecurity Insight shows that since fighting started between the SAF and RSF in April 2023 and 30 September 2026, 807 attacks on health care took place across Sudan, with over 55% of attacks attributed to the RSF. Health facilities were attacked and damaged at least 232 times while 222 health workers were killed, 144 arrested and 16 kidnapped.
Since January 2026, reported incidents in the Kordofan states accounted for 51% of all incidents, reflecting the intensification of fighting between conflict parties and the threats to health care delivery, health workers and patients. In the first nine months of 2026, 45 incidents affecting health care were reported across the three Kordofan states, exceeding the annual totals recorded in 2025 (35), 2024 (7) and 2023 (24). Of the 45 incidents in 2026, 51% recorded aircraft or drone strikes which damaged health facilities and killed and injured health workers. Most of these incidents were attributed to the RSF, particularly in North Kordofan, and to RSF-allied groups, including the Sudan People's Liberation Movement-North, in South Kordofan. Incidents attributed to the SAF were less frequent and occurred predominantly in West Kordofan. Health facilities were also looted and on at least one occasion, forcibly repurposed, further undermining access to essential health care for civilians. In one such incident in West Kordofan, the RSF reportedly converted a hospital into a field hospital to treat its own members, forcibly evacuating patients in the process, depriving civilians of access to critical medical care at a time when conflict-related needs are acute and health services are already under severe strain.

--- Report 2: UNFPA (2026-11-09) ---
In September, conflict in Blue Nile State intensified and spread to the southern parts of the state, displacing 52,629 people and placing further strain on health, protection and other essential services. Nearly 12,000 displaced people are sheltering in severely overcrowded schools with limited or no access to sanitation, electricity and other basic services.
Insecurity across the three Kordofan States, including drone strikes and fighting around El Obeid, Bara, Dilling, Nuhud and Abu Zabad, continued to drive displacement. According to the Sudan Health Cluster, cholera outbreak also persisted, with 1,501 cases and 92 deaths reported in North Kordofan and 1,299 cases and 115 deaths in South Kordofan as of 30 September. Safety audits and inter-agency assessments in North Darfur, North Kordofan and West Darfur found that gender-based violence (GBV) risks remain severe and continue to intensify.
UNFPA’s 2026 appeal of US$129.2 million is only around 40 per cent funded, leaving a gap of US$78 million, and current response efforts remain insufficient to meet the needs of women and girls. In September, UNFPA and partners delivered more than 145,000 sexual and reproductive health services across 17 states via healthcare facilities and mobile teams. Additionally, UNFPA reached some 93,000 people with essential GBV prevention and response services, including safe spaces, psychosocial support, and dignity kit distributions.

--- Report 3: ETC (2026-11-09) ---
Summary Points
• LTC Telecoms sustained internet connectivity for approximately 1,700 users across 70 humanitarian locations, supporting humanitarian communications, coordination and response activities across Sudan.
• Five Security Operations Centres (SOCs) remained operational in Port Sudan, Kassala, El Gedaref, Kosti and Damazine, while LTC Telecoms continued providing radio communications support to humanitarian partners.
• LTC Telecoms continued community connectivity services at the Al-Aim Community Centre in Khartoum, where 392 people accessed data connectivity and phone-charging services during October, while joint assessments with WFP in Kosti and El Obeid explored opportunities to expand telecoms services.
