Summary Points
• Despite funding shortfalls and operational constraints, LTC Telecoms maintained internet connectivity services for approximately 1,700 users across 70 humanitarian locations, enabling critical communications, coordination, and response activities across Sudan.

--- Report 4: Protection Cluster (2026-09-30) ---
The alert was issued following the drone strikes of 25 September 2026, which reportedly affected several civilian locations in El Obeid, including a shelter hosting displaced families, a fuel station, a civilian passenger bus and a residential house. At least two civilians were reportedly killed and around 20 others injured. The incident forms part of a wider escalation across North Kordofan and a recurring pattern of drone attacks affecting civilians, displaced populations and essential civilian infrastructure in and around El Obeid.

--- Report 5: WFP (2026-09-29) ---

18,200 mt food distributed in August 2026
USD 7.7 million cash transferred in August 2026
USD 591 million required in the next six months (September 2026-February 2027)
KEY HIGHLIGHTS

In August, WFP reached a record 4.1 million people across Sudan, including 1 million people in areas classified as at Risk of Famine (RoF), underscoring the scale and reach of the operation in the country's most vulnerable locations.
August was the third consecutive month of record-breaking reach in 2026, reflecting sustained progress in overcoming access and operational challenges.
Cash-based assistance reached 1.6 million people, the highest monthly reach supported through cash transfers since the conflict began.
School feeding activities reached 218,000 schoolchildren, contributing to the recovery of essential education support services and helping children remain engaged in learning.
Combined, Darfur and Kordofan remained the centre of WFP's operational response, accounting for nearly three-quarters of all beneficiaries reached across Sudan during the month.
