KEY HIGHLIGHTS

In August, WFP reached a record 4.1 million people across Sudan, including 1 million people in areas classified as at Risk of Famine (RoF), underscoring the scale and reach of the operation in the country's most vulnerable locations.
August was the third consecutive month of record-breaking reach in 2026, reflecting sustained progress in overcoming access and operational challenges.
Cash-based assistance reached 1.6 million people, the highest monthly reach supported through cash transfers since the conflict began.
School feeding activities reached 218,000 schoolchildren, contributing to the recovery of essential education support services and helping children remain engaged in learning.
Combined, Darfur and Kordofan remained the centre of WFP's operational response, accounting for nearly three-quarters of all beneficiaries reached across Sudan during the month.
