Crisis / situation: Sudan

--- Report 1: WFP (2026-07-24) ---
KEY HIGHLIGHTS

In June, WFP reached 3.7 million people, the highest monthly number in 2026, driven by WFP’s strong logistical and procurement capacity, ability to access hard-to-reach areas, close coordination with partners and government counterparts, and the scale-up of both in-kind food assistance and cash-based transfers (CBT).
Of all the people reached, 1 million received assistance in areas classified as at Risk of Famine (RoF).
WFP reached 1.4 million people through CBT in June, also the highest monthly reach achieved since the onset of the conflict.
WFP is facing severe funding constraints. Without additional resources, WFP's food stocks are expected to be exhausted by September, while funding for lifesaving CBT assistance is projected to run out in August, leaving millions of vulnerable people without support.

--- Report 2: Protection Cluster (2026-07-23) ---
On 20 July 2026, local sources reported that three drones struck multiple locations in Al Rahad, North Kordofan.
The reported strikes impacted a health centre and an area adjacent to the main market during peak morning activity.
While casualty figures remain under verification, UNICEF subsequently reported tha at least 2 children were killed and 6 children, aged between 6 and 14, were injured. Local sources also reported that 4 adults were killed, and more than 20 others were injured.
The incident followed a reported attack on Al Musallamiya village on 19 July 2026, in which local sources reported that 13 civilians were killed.
On21 July 2026, local sources reported that in Al Hugeina village, approximately 30 km from Al Rahad a drone strike allegedly targeted a girls' primary school which is still under verification at the time of reporting. One teacher was reportedly injured.
