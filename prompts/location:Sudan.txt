Crisis / situation: Sudan

--- Report 1: ETC (2026-11-09) ---
Summary Points
• LTC Telecoms sustained internet connectivity for approximately 1,700 users across 70 humanitarian locations, supporting humanitarian communications, coordination and response activities across Sudan.
• Five Security Operations Centres (SOCs) remained operational in Port Sudan, Kassala, El Gedaref, Kosti and Damazine, while LTC Telecoms continued providing radio communications support to humanitarian partners.
• LTC Telecoms continued community connectivity services at the Al-Aim Community Centre in Khartoum, where 392 people accessed data connectivity and phone-charging services during October, while joint assessments with WFP in Kosti and El Obeid explored opportunities to expand telecoms services.
