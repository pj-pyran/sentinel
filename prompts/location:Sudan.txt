Crisis / situation: Sudan

--- Report 1: IOM (2026-10-29) ---
Between 11 January and 15 October 2026, an estimated 119,782 individuals (23,598 households) were displaced from locations across Blue Nile state.

A total of 119,782 IDPs marks a 21 per cent increase compared to the previous update, up from 99,277 IDPs recorded on 23 August 2026.
Individuals were displaced from locations across Geisan (40,090 IDPs), Al Kurmuk (31,165 IDPs), Baw (27,192 IDPs), and Wad Al Mahi (21,335 IDPs) localities.
Individuals were displaced to seven localities in Blue Nile, including locations across Ed Damazine (45,815 IDPs), Ar Rusayris (36,950 IDPs), Baw (12,687 IDPs), Wad Al Mahi (11,685 IDPs), Geisan (10,275 IDPs), Al Kurmuk (1,385 IDPs), and At Tadamon (985 IDPs).
While the overall IDP population in Blue Nile primarily stayed with host families (55%), newly displaced households were more likely to reside at informal gathering sites (56%) or in schools and other public buildings (20%).
Approximately 56 per cent of IDPs were female, compared to 44 per cent male. Nearly half (45%) were children, under the age of 18.

For further information on displacement across Sudan, see DTM Sudan Displacement and Return Snapshot (10). DTM reports on all events triggering displacement within Sudan via Early Warning Flash Alerts.

--- Report 2: UNICEF (2026-10-29) ---
Highlights

Despite nearly 5 million returns reducing Sudan’s overall IDP population, escalating conflict in Blue Nile, Darfur and Kordofan displaced more than 58,000 people in August alone.
UNICEF supported health interventions reached 751,400 people with cholera outbreak response and 769,900 people following a diphtheria vaccination campaign in high-risk localities. UNICEF supported the treatment of 51,506 children with Severe Acute Malnutrition and provided IYCF counselling to 183,264 caregivers. UNICEF also enabled 271,000 people to access safe water and reached 44,400 people with critical WASH supplies.
With UNICEF support, 14,600 children accessed education opportunities, and 57,200 received learning materials. UNICEF also reached 112,700 people with critical Mental Health and Psychosocial Support services and provided Gender-Based Violence services to 84,000 people.
As of 31 August 2026, UNICEF had mobilized US$352.2 million against US$962.9 million HAC appeal (63 per cent funding gap). Increased flexible funding is urgently required, particularly for critically underfunded child protection programme.
