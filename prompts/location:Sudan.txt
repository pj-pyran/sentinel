Crisis / situation: Sudan

--- Report 1: Insecurity Insight (2026-04-21) ---
Insecurity Insight identified 735 incidents of violence against or obstruction of access to health care in Sudan since fighting started between the Sudan Armed Forces (SAF) and Rapid Support Forces (RSF) in April 2023 and 31 March 2026. At least 195 health workers were killed and 126 arrested, while health facilities were reportedly damaged on 205 occasions. Most of these 735 incidents were attributed to the RSF. --- Report 2: UNHCR (2026-04-20) ---
Current Humanitarian Situation Despite repeated diplomatic efforts to achieve a ceasefire, the conflict in Sudan between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) has continued unabated since April 2023 and has become the world’s largest displacement and protection crisis. The conflict in Sudan has been marked by severe violence and widespread human rights violations against civilians, including sexual violence, torture, arbitrary killings, extortion, and the targeting of specific ethnic groups—resulting in a humanitarian crisis across the country and the wider region.
