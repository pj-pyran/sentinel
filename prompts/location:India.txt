Crisis / situation: India

--- Report 1: HAI (2026-07-28) ---
CURRENT SITUATION

LOCAL has verified 26,400 families in urgent need (21,000 families in Sivasagar, 2,400 families in Jorhat and 3,000 families across Golaghat and Charaideo districts)
The death toll has risen to 68. Over 5.24 lakh people remain affected across Charaideo, Golaghat, Jorhat, Nagaon and Sivasagar. Sivasagar is now the worstaffected district with nearly 1.9 lakh people impacted. 354 relief camps and distribution centres are operational across four districts, sheltering 37,724 people.
Floodwaters are gradually receding across Upper Assam. The focus has shifted to relief distribution, power restoration and medical deployment, with air-drops continuing in inaccessible areas. However, breached embankments and shallow waters are hampering both boat and vehicle access in several villages.
As waters recede, a public health crisis is emerging in areas like Dergaon, with stagnant water, waste, snake infestations and disease risk. Residents are urgently calling for disinfectants, bleaching powder and health camps.

--- Report 2: Sphere India (2026-07-27) ---
SITUATION UPDATE
A. Situation Overview:
India is experiencing multi-state monsoon emergencies across Assam, Jammu &amp; Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Gujarat and Maharashtra (Palghar District). Among all affected regions, Assam remains the worst-hit, with widespread flooding, massive displacement, and extensive crop and livestock losses.
1. Assam Flood - Severe Flooding Across 12 Districts
Assam is facing one of its most severe flood waves in recent years, with 12 districts, 856 villages, and over 7 lakh people affected. Sivasagar, Jorhat, Charaideo and Golaghat are the worst-hit. Human casualties have reached 14, and livestock losses exceed 3.34 lakh, with massive crop damage across 56,600 hectares. Relief camps are sheltering 115,205 people, while another 2 lakh depend on external relief. The humanitarian, agricultural, and infrastructure impacts are extensive and escalating.
