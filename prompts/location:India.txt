Crisis / situation: India

--- Report 1: WFP (2026-08-11) ---
KEY HIGHLIGHTS
• To promote climate resilience approaches and strengthen community capacities, in collaboration with the Assam Agriculture University, over 60 plant clinics have been organised at the Climate Adaptation Information Centre. Under the ENACT-Assam project, the clinics connect smallholder farmers with experts to help protect diversified livelihoods.
SITUATION OVERVIEW
• Despite decades of gains in food production and nutrition, India continues to face significant food and nutrition insecurity. It ranks 102nd out of 123 countries on the 2025 Global Hunger Index, with malnutrition indicators remaining above acceptable thresholds: 35 percent of children under five are stunted, 19 percent are underweight, and anaemia affects 57 percent of women of reproductive age and 67 percent of children aged 6–59 months. The State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World, released in July 2026, demonstrated India’s significant improvements in the prevalence of undernourishment and the affordability of a healthy diet.
• India operates some of the world’s largest food-based safety nets, reaching nearly 800 million people every month. WFP has partnered with the Government of India for over 60 years, providing technical assistance and capacity strengthening to enhance efficiency, nutrition quality, digital innovation, and climate resilience across the food system.

--- Report 2: UNHCR (2026-08-11) ---
Operational Context
India continues to be a generous host to refugees and asylum-seekers from across the region and beyond. UNHCR works in close coordination with the Government of India and other UN agencies in fulfilling its mandate of ensuring access to protection, solutions, and humanitarian assistance to persons forced to flee.
Civil society organizations, working in partnership with UNHCR, are at the forefront of the refugee response ensuring that assistance reaches the most vulnerable within the overall objective of “leaving no one behind”.
Partnership agreements are in place with five national NGOs.
UNHCR works closely with the Government of India and the UN Country Team to ensure that refugees and asylum‑seekers are included in national efforts to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals, in line with the principle of leaving no one behind
