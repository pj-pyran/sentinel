--- Report 2: Sphere India (2026-10-29) ---
SITUATION UPDATE
A. Situation Overview (28–29 October 2026)
Very Severe Cyclonic Storm Arnab crossed the Andhra Pradesh coast near Narasapuram (West Godavari). The landfall process began at about 01:30 IST on 28 October, with sustained winds of 125–135 km/h gusting to 150 km/h. IMD forecast a storm surge of 1.5–2.5 m over low-lying Kakinada and Yanam. North coastal AP and south Odisha were hit hardest by rain. The system is moving NNW inland and weakening.

--- Report 3: HAI (2026-10-29) ---
CURRENT SITUATION

Severe Cyclonic Storm Arnab completed landfall southwest of Yanam, Andhra Pradesh, on the morning of 28 October. The system has weakened to a severe cyclonic storm and is forecast to further weaken into a depression by 29 October as it moves inland over southern Odisha and Chhattisgarh.
Five deaths have been reported in Andhra Pradesh in cyclone-related incidents.
Government Preliminary assessments indicated that around 180 houses had been damaged, while more than 220 trees had fallen across roads, disrupting transport and movement in several areas.
LOCAL member reports indicate waterlogging, fallen trees and disrupted sanitation in Gopalpur and Rangeilunda blocks, with approximately 10–15 houses reportedly damaged.
In Baxipalli ODRP, 42 households were reportedly affected by flooding, six roofs were lost and 12 people were rescued.
Flooding has also been reported in ODRP Colony, Podampeta.
In Andhra Pradesh, approximately 89,000 people were being evacuated to 101 relief camps as of 27 October. Kakinada district reported more than 10,000 people accommodated in around 495 shelters;5 while 10,176 households across 37 villages in Visakhapatnam district were identified as being at risk;
In Odisha, 47,830 people were reported evacuated across eight districts; The largest planned evacuation numbers were in Nabarangpur (11,200), Gajapati (11,000) and Malkangiri (9,850).
Red warnings for extremely heavy rainfall were issued for Gajapati and Ganjam districts in Odisha.
The IMD bulletin also indicated flash-flood risk across 35 districts—15 in Andhra Pradesh, 11 in Odisha and nine in Chhattisgarh.
Fisherfolk were advised to remain ashore until 29 October
Fallen trees have reportedly blocked roads in Anakapalli, Visakhapatnam, Kakinada and Konaseema districts.14,15 The Uppada coastal road has also reportedly been washed away
