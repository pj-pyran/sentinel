Crisis / situation: Nigeria

--- Report 1: IOM (2026-09-16) ---
On 23 August 2026, an armed banditry and kidnapping incident occurred in Sauna community in Sauna R/Gora ward of Talata Mafara Local Government Area, Zamfara State.
According to key informants, the incident displaced 531 individuals from 121 households, including 297 children, 217 adults, and 17 elderly persons. Of the displaced population, 531 individuals moved from Sauna community to Tsakkuwa community in Shiyar Galadima ward of Talata Mafara Local Government Area, where they are currently residing within the host community. A total of nine injuries were reported.
Following the incident, Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) field staff conducted rapid assessments on 24 August 2026 to inform the humanitarian community, as well as the government and partners, and to enable a more targeted response. Non-food items (NFIs), shelter and food were identified as priority needs.

--- Report 2: IOM (2026-09-16) ---
Between 19 and 23 August 2026, armed bandits attacked the communities of Unguwar Guru in Sukuntuni ward of Kankia Local Government Area (LGA) and Nasarawa in Dantankari ward of Dandume LGA, both in Katsina State.
According to key informants, the attacks affected 4,727 individuals across 607 households and displaced 3,601 individuals from 456 households, including 2,564 children, 559 women, and 478 men. Of the displaced population, 3,565 individuals moved from Unguwar Guru to Dokoki in Rimaye ward of Kankia LGA and 36 individuals moved from Nasarawa to Dandume town in Dandume A ward of Dandume LGA. The incidents also resulted in 102 casualties, comprising 88 injuries and 14 fatalities.
Following the incidents, Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) field staff conducted rapid assessments on 23 August 2026 to inform the humanitarian community, as well as the government and partners, to enable a more targeted response. Food was the most prevalent need identified. Other reported needs included Non-food items (NFIs), water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH), education and protection.
