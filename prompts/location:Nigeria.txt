Crisis / situation: Nigeria

--- Report 1: IOM (2026-08-04) ---
On 25, 27 and 28 July 2026, armed bandits attacked the communities of Magaji Wando in Magaji Wando B ward, Bokolo and Nasarawai in Dandume A ward of Dandume Local Government Area (LGA), Katsina State. According to key informants, the attack displaced 252 individuals from 38 households, including 139 children, 66 women, and 47 men.
Following the incident, Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) field staff conducted rapid assessments on 29 and 30 July 2026 to inform the humanitarian community, as well as the government and partners, to enable a more targeted response

KEY HIGHLIGHTS
• Conflict, displacement and restricted access to farmland continue to drive food insecurity outcomes across northern Nigeria during the lean season as irregular rainfall disrupts agricultural production and raises concerns
• An OCHA-led inter-sector severity classification update, informed by Cadre Harmonisé findings and other multi-sector data to guide humanitarian prioritization has identified 15 LGAs as Severity Level 4.
• WFP assistance reached more people in June compared to the previous month, providing nutrition-integrated food assistance and strengthening resilience and food systems.
