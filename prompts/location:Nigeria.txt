KEY HIGHLIGHTS
• Funding constraints continue to affect WFP’s operational capacity amid high food needs. A critical funding gap for general food assistance is projected in October 2026, forcing WFP to suspend interventions.
• WFP reached 1.1 million people in July. This includes general food assistance for 908,838 people across Borno, Yobe, Katsina and Sokoto states.
• The IDP camp in Gwoza Local Government Areas was closed in July as part of the Borno State Government’s durable solutions to internal displacement. This follows the closure of the Bama camp that occurred in June.

--- Report 2: IOM (2026-09-12) ---
On 16 August 2026, armed bandits attacked the communities of Galko, Baruda, Tudun Boshi, Gidan Illo, Gulle, Fass, Gusura and Yar Gusau, all in Bardoki ward of Gummi Local Government Area (LGA), Zamfara State. According to key informants, the attacks displaced 14,727 individuals across 2,458 households, including 8,240 children, 4,248 women, and 2,239 men.

--- Report 3: IOM (2026-09-12) ---
Between 9 and 14 August 2026, armed bandits attacked the communities of Labo Kankia in Kurami Yankwani ward of Bakori Local Government Area (LGA), Magaji Wondo Dansuda in Magaji Wondo ward, Gyazamar and Bokolo in Dandume A ward, Unguwar Rimin Jiruwa in Dandume B ward of Dandume LGA and Daddara in Kusa ward of Jibia LGA, all in Katsina State. According to key informants, the attacks affected 2,723 individuals across 629 households and displaced 1,535 individuals from 218 households, including 697 children, 495 women, and 343 men.
