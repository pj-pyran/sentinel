Crisis / situation: Sri Lanka: Dengue Outbreak - May 2026

--- Report 1: Govt. Sri Lanka (2026-07-22) ---
DENGUE CASE REPORTING
During the 29th week, 7385 suspected dengue cases were reported across all 26 districts, compared to 7619 cases in the previous week, reflecting an 3.1% decrease. The Western Province accounted for 52.4% of total cases, with the Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) contributing 3.6%, the rest of Colombo District 17.1%, Gampaha District 24.6%, and Kalutara District 7.1%. Outside the Western Province, notable case contributions were reported from Kandy (8.9%), Matara (6.1%), Ratnapura (4.7%), Galle (4.4%), Kurunegala (3.5%), Puttalam (3.1%) Kegalle (3%), and Hambantota (2.4%) (see Table 1) (compared to the previous week, colour of %: red=increase, green=decrease, blue=no change).
