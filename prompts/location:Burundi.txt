Crisis / situation: Burundi

--- Report 1: WFP (2026-05-21) ---
Food assistance distribution: In April 2026, WFP successfully assisted close to 1 million people, distributing 181 mt of food and USD 3.1 million in cash transfers.
Fragile humanitarian situation during lean season: In April 2026, Burundi faced the lean season with reduced food supplies, rising bean prices, and added pressures from refugee movements and climate shocks—highlighting a fragile humanitarian situation and the urgent need for sustained humanitarian support and stronger social protection systems.
Congolese refugees voluntary return: On 23 April 2026, UNHCR and the Government of Burundi launched voluntary returns of Congolese refugees under the Tripartite Framework. However, the scale of repatriation during April remained limited, with minimal effect on the overall refugee situation.

--- Report 2: Croix-Rouge du Burundi (2026-05-19) ---
Sources for data marked as Other
The figures presented here come from the 2024 general population census. Indeed, the entire province of Bujumbura, comprising the former provinces of Mairie, Bubanza, Bujumbura, and Cibitoke, borders the DRC and has entry and exit points, making it a high-risk zone for the spread of the Ebola epidemic. The large city of Bujumbura, with approximately 50% of the province's population, is thus more exposed and more vulnerable should the disease reach it.

Activation / Mobilization of Volunteers
Summary
Following the announcement of the official government statement prohibiting travel to Burundi and calling for preventive measures, the Burundian Red Crescent (BRC) activated its extensive network of volunteers for surveillance and early warning of any suspected cases. The Task Force is operational at headquarters and in branches. A situation report has been posted on the IFRC's Go platform.
