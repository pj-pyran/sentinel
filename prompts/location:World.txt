--- Report 1: UNHCR (2026-06-16) ---
1. Monthly analysis: By April 2026, total recorded movements across mixed movement routes sharply diverged. The Central Mediterranean Route and Eastern Mediterranean Route remained below 2025 levels (–46 per cent and –32 per cent year-to-date respectively), while the South and South-East Asia route more than doubled compared to the same period last year (+152 per cent year-to-date). The Western Mediterranean Route also expanded (+75 per cent year-to-date), contrasting with the West African Atlantic Route, which remains deeply contracted (–78 per cent year-to-date) despite a short-term rebound in April. For more detailed information on monthly / weekly trends, please refer to A Route-Based Snapshot: Data &amp; Trends for Refugees from Myanmar (end April 2026) , Situation Europe Sea Arrivals and Italy Monthly Sea Arrivals (Update April 2026).

--- Report 2: UNHCR (2026-06-16) ---
HIGHLIGHTS


UNHCR and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs commemorated 45 years of partnership, recognizing joint efforts to strengthen protection for forcibly displaced and stateless people. The event brought together government authorities, UN agencies, civil society, academia, and international partners, reaffirming a shared commitment to dignity, protection, and solutions. UNHCR highlighted milestones, including enhancing the capacities of the Commission for Refugee Status Determination (CODER), and presented a recognition to the country.
The ceremony also marked the opening of the art exhibition “Solidarity with People Forced to Flee,” developed in partnership with students from the Dr. José Matías Delgado University, which raises awareness about forced displacement and promotes solidarity for people seeking international protection.


UNHCR joined the United Nations Resident Coordinator and the General Directorate of Civil Protection to launch the Humanitarian Assistance Logistics System (SILAH). Developed with UNHCR’s support, SILAH supports ongoing efforts to digitalize the management and distribution of humanitarian assistance, contributing to improved response during emergencies and disasters. The launch event brought together the Vice Minister of Foreign Affairs, members of the Humanitarian Country Team, and representatives of national institutions.


As the 2026 MIRPS Pro Tempore Presidency, El Salvador advanced efforts to update its national pledges and strengthen regional coordination. UNHCR supported the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and member institutions throughout the process, including during the first MIRPS Plenary of the year, where participating countries endorsed regional commitments
