--- Report 1: UNHCR (2026-11-26) ---
Highlights
1. The Central Mediterranean Route recorded a moderate increase in September, driven by sea arrivals to Italy. Italy registered 3,329 arrivals, 11% more than in August, but 60% fewer than in September 2025. Nationality patterns also shifted : compared with September 2025, when Bangladeshis and Egyptians predominated, the September 2026 profile was led by Algerians, Eritreans, Bangladeshis and Somalis.
2. Arrivals to Spain increased in September, driven by a rise in arrivals to the Canary Islands in the Western Africa Atlantic Route. Spain recorded 4,229 arrivals during the month, 27% more than in August and 24% more than in September 2025. Arrivals along the Western Africa Atlantic Route increased from 488 in August to 1,970 in September, while arrivals to mainland Spain and the Bale aric Islands along the Western Mediterranean Route declined from 2,832 to 2,259. In Algeria, disembarkations following interceptions or rescue operations increased moderately, from 686 in August to 788 in September, but remained below the 1,405 recorded in September 2025. Overall, Spain recorded 24,646 sea and land arrivals between January and September 2026, 10% fewer than during the same period in 2025.
3. Maritime movements along the South and South -East Asia Route reached their highest level for the first nine months of any year since systematic tracking began in 2022. Around 6,000 maritime movements were recorded between January and September 2026, alongside an estimated 10,700 land movements. The majority of maritime arrivals occurred along routes between Myanmar and Malaysia, while most estimated land movements were t owards Bangladesh .

Africa
Central African Republic
01 October 2026: In Bar village, Zemio sub prefecture, Haut-Mbomou prefecture, an unspecified number of women and girls were taken away and sexually assaulted by Wagner Group and their auxiliaries, the &quot;Black Russians' during the evening and throughout the night. The perpetrators had stormed the village in the early hours of the morning, beating and injuring some civilians. Source: Corbeau News
Kenya
09 October 2026: In Imenti Central sub-county, Meru county, a three-and-a-half-year-old girl was sexually assaulted by suspected police officers at the Kinjo Police Patrol Base. Following the attack, the child was taken to Nairobi Women’s Hospital in Meru where doctors conducted a medical examination. On 11 November, two officers were taken into custody. Source: People Daily
Nigeria
As reported on 08 October 2026: In an undisclosed location in Kaduna state, a woman was reportedly sexually abused by a Kaduna militia after they reportedly abducted her to facilitate a ransom demand for the release of a suspect arrested by police forces. The victim was killed after they collected the ransom. Source: Punch NG

India
10-11 October 2026: In Delhi city and state, three journalists were sexually harassed by police officers during a protest calling for the resignation of the country’s top election official. Source: Human Rights Watch
29 October 2026: In Saraikela-Kharsawan district, Saraikela town, Jharkhand state, a woman was raped by a police constable inside Gamharia police station after going there to seek police assistance after a dispute with her husband. The constable was arrested and five police personnel were suspended for negligence. Source: The New Indian Express

Syrian Arab Republic
11 October 2026: In Hama Hama governorate, a 15-year-old girl from the Alawite sect from Tal Sekkin village was sexually assaulted after being lured away in the evening by a suspected member of the General Security forces along with suspected local individuals . She was later found at the junction of Al-Hemeiri village in a critical physical and psychological condition. There were accusations of attempts to cover up the case, intimidate the girl's family and some villagers, and detain some of them. Source: SOHR
