Crisis / situation: World

--- Report 1: WHO (2026-05-13) ---
Major issues and challenges include Mpox in Guinea: The mpox outbreak in Guinea remains persistent with ongoing low-level transmission across urban districts, reflecting incomplete interruption of transmission chains despite sustained response efforts. The response is constrained by suboptimal contact tracing effectiveness, centralized case management capacity, and continued transmission among identified contacts, limiting early detection and timely isolation of cases. These challenges are compounded by population movement within densely populated areas of Conakry and surrounding districts, increasing the risk of continued spread and prolonging the outbreak. Addressing these gaps will require strengthening decentralized case management, improving the timeliness and completeness of contact follow-up, and intensifying targeted interventions in high-transmission urban settings.
Humanitarian Crisis in Burundi: The response to the humanitarian crisis in Burundi remains constrained by limited capacity in overcrowded reception and refugee sites, resource gaps, and increasing pressure on host communities, limiting the effective delivery of essential services. These challenges are compounded by rising food insecurity and population movements, increasing the risk of disease outbreaks and worsening nutritional outcomes. Addressing these constraints will require scaling up resource mobilization, expanding service capacity in high-burden areas, and strengthening integrated humanitarian and public health response.

--- Report 2: Insecurity Insight (2026-05-13) ---
Insecurity Insight’s Protection in Danger open-source monitoring identified 8,527 incidents of threats and violence that took place at IDP/refugee camps, including temporary/makeshift shelters between 01 January 2020 and March 2026. In these incidents, camps were damaged or destroyed 5,120 times, 6,320 camp residents were killed, 13,153 injured, 473 kidnapped and 5,144 arrested. Almost 55% of incidents reported Israeli aircraft and drone strikes, artillery shelling and sniper attacks on refugee and makeshift camps in Gaza between October 2023 and March 2026.
