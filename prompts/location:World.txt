--- Report 3: World Vision (2026-10-05) ---
Cambodia is experiencing significant flooding, with the National Committee for Disaster Management (NCDM) reporting impacts across 10 provinces, 53 districts/cities and 255 communes/sangkats, affecting 73,922 households as of 29 September 2026.
In World Vision Cambodia’s operational areas, 2,535 households, including 3,066 children among 7,919 affected people, have been directly impacted, with flooding also disrupting livelihoods, education and access to safe water and sanitation.
World Vision is mobilising resources to provide urgent assistance in water, sanitation and hygiene, food, shelter, child protection, education and other essential needs. Working alongside local authorities and partners, we aim to protect the most vulnerable children and families and support their recovery with dignity and hope.
