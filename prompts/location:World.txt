Crisis / situation: World

--- Report 1: UNHCR (2026-07-23) ---
Highlights
1. In May 2026, the Routes Monitor recorded 9,218 arrivals , 4,488 disembarkations and 13,706 reconstructed departures across five covered routes. Compared with April 2026, arrivals increased by 30 per cent, disembarkations by 54 per cent, and departures by 37 per cent. Compared with May 2025, all three indicators remained lower overall.

--- Report 2: FAO (2026-07-22) ---
El Niño forcing continues to dominate the seasonal precipitation forecasts, and the outlook is nearly unchanged from last month, with minor changes in the predicted precipitation anomalies in a few regions. First, the dry signal has intensified over western India and southeastern Pakistan in August and September, reflecting both the stronger El Niño signal and a slightly less positive Indian Ocean Dipole phase. Wet conditions are still expected to develop across the Horn of Africa in September, extending to northern and eastern Africa and the Middle East in October, with stronger August–October dryness in India and Pakistan, while wetter conditions are expected in western Africa. Another minor shift is a stronger wet signal across the northern Sahel from September through November, with the notable wet anomaly likely indicating a delayed end to the rainy season. Summer breeding will occur in Mauritania and, at a smaller scale, in other Sahelian areas. Breeding should continue in the Western Region during August and September, increasing the risk of outbreaks from September onwards.
