--- Report 1: Insecurity Insight (2026-10-29) ---
Aid in Danger incidents affecting aid agencies and their staff and impact on programmes
01-15 October 2026
Incidents of threats and violence affecting aid workers, aid delivery and aid impact supporting aid agencies in risk mitigation and safety and security measures when implementing programmes. The incidents reported are not a complete nor a representative list of all events that affected the provision of aid delivery. Insecurity Insight continues to update data and figures may change. Updated data includes new and historic reports identified in open-sources and verified security incidents submitted by Aid in Danger partner agencies.
Africa
Democratic Republic of the Congo
01 October 2026: Update: An ambulance driver with the Uganda Red Cross Society, died in Kampala city of Uganda, from injuries sustained in an attack while carrying out humanitarian duties related to Ebola response in Aru town, Ituri province. He had been airlifted to Kampala for specialised medical care. Source: Pulse
As reported on 02 October 2026: In Ituri province, escalating violence disrupted humanitarian operations, cutting people off from receiving aid, and affecting efforts to contain the Ebola outbreak. Source: UN News
As reported on 02 October 2026: In Djugu territory, at least five humanitarian organisations suspended or limited operations and relocated staff, disrupting access to food, healthcare, nutrition services, cash assistance, and water and sanitation support. Sources: UN News and UN News I
Ethiopia
04 October 2026: In Tigray region, a UN agency warehouse, and facilities belonging to local and international NGOs, were looted and humanitarian supplies were stolen, including food, seed, fertilisers and water pumps. Source: UN News
14 October 2026: In Juraysis Mender, Dangur woreda, Metekel zone, Benishangul-Gumuz region, an Ethiopian Red Cross Society ambulance was attacked by an armed group while carrying out a humanitarian mission. The ambulance driver was killed, and the ambulance was severely damaged and rendered inoperable. Source: Ethiopian Red Cross Society
Sudan
02 October 2026: In Dilling city, South Kordofan state, a World Food Programme (WFP) truck was hit by an explosive device of unidentified origin after delivering life-saving food aid. Source: Sudan Nile
03 October 2026: In Dilling–Kadugli road, South Kordofan state, two clearly-marked trucks contracted by the WPF and transporting food aid from Dilling to Kadugli were hit by an airstrike carried out by an unidentified armed group, killing a driver and damaging food supplies. The extent of damage to the food shipment was still being verified, and the WFP called for a prompt and impartial investigation into the attack. Sources: Al Taghyeer,  Radio Tamazuj and UN News
As reported on 04 October 2026: In Kutum locality, North Darfur state, 32 trucks loaded with humanitarian aid belonging to the WFP were detained by RSF after they intercepted a convoy of 44 trucks and forcibly took them to El Fasher. After three days, 12 trucks were released, while the remaining 32 continued to be detained after the RSF demanded large sums of money for their release, obstructing the delivery of food assistance intended for displaced families in North Darfur. Source: Al Nilin

SHCC Attacks on Health Care
The section aligns with the definition of attacks on health care used by the Safeguarding Health in Conflict Coalition (SHCC).
Africa
Democratic Republic of the Congo
12-13 October 2026: In Jissa locality, Djugu territory, Ituri province, a health centre was set on fire and looted by suspected CODECO militia, who stole pharmaceutical products and furniture from the facility. The facility supports the care of over 5,000 people. Sources: Actualite and UN News

--- Report 3: Insecurity Insight (2026-10-29) ---
Reporting Sexual Violence
By state bodies or conflict actors that particularly targets IDPs and refugees, aid, health workers or educators or students among others.
Africa
Central African Republic
21-25 September 2026: In Mbomou prefecture, six cases of gender-based violence (GBV) by an unidentified perpetrator were reported. Two GBV survivors were referred to Rafaï Hospital for medical care. Source: OCHA Situation Report No. 87
South Africa
03 September 2026: In Amatikwe village, Inanda township, KwaZulu-Natal province, a 15-year-old girl was raped by a police officer after leaving her home on 27 August 2026, she was offered a place to stay by the officer. The sergeant was arrested and appeared at the Ntuzuma Magistrate’s Court on 09 September. Sources: IOL and Weekend Argus
South Sudan
28 September 2026: Between Lui and Lanyi Dawa, Mundri East county, Equatoria state, an unspecified number of women were raped during a road ambush of a bus driving along the road from Juba to Mundri, by an unidentified armed group. At least 32 people were abducted in the attack. Source: Radio Tamazuj
Nigeria
20 September 2026: In Jalingo, Taraba state, a female student was raped by members of an unidentified armed group during an overnight attack on a female hostel of the Taraba State College of Nursing Sciences. The hostel was located outside the institution's main premises. Several other students were injured, and valuables, including mobile phones were taken. The attack triggered a peaceful protest by hundreds of students. Source: Daily Post
Sudan
As reported on 05 September 2026: In El Fasher city, North Darfur state, the UN confirmed that senior commanders of the RSF directly ordered fighters to commit rape and killings during the October 2025 take over of El Fasher. Source: Sudan Tribune
