--- Report 1: FAO (2026-08-20) ---
The rapid intensification of El Niño has continued in the recent weeks, and the episode is already the strongest on record for this time of year, while the positive Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD) is expected to persist in the coming months. In September, above-normal rainfall is forecast from northern Africa to the central Middle East, with wetter conditions also expected in eastern Ethiopia and Somalia due to the positive IOD. A dry signal may remain over India and Pakistan toward the close of the monsoon season. During October and November, the El Niño-related wet signal is expected to expand across northern and eastern Africa and the Middle East. Interior eastern Africa, the southern Arabian Peninsula, and Pakistan are likely to become wetter than normal. However, northwestern Africa may turn drier than normal by November. Overall, the forecast is consistent with rainfall patterns observed during previous strong El Niño events, with generally good model agreement. Some uncertainty remains over the northern Sahel and the strength of the IOD.

--- Report 2: Insecurity Insight (2026-08-18) ---
This News Brief bears testimony to the brave survivors who speak about sexual violence by state bodies or conflict actors. Most events of violence are never reported. This compilation is neither complete nor representative of the extent or nature of sexual violence in general. It brings together dispersed accounts about survivors from around the world who broke the silence.
Developments in July 2026
Central African Republic: In July 2026, reported incidents in Ouham-Fafa and Vakaga highlight the exposure of women and girls to sexual violence amid insecurity and armed-group activity, particularly in remote and rural communities. The incidents, involving unidentified armed men and LRA fighters, reflect recurring trends of sexual violence by multiple armed actors, heightened risks for women and girls while carrying out everyday livelihood activities, and limited access to protection and essential services in isolated, conflict-affected areas. See the Central African Republic section for more details.
Democratic Republic of the Congo: In July 2026, reported incidents in Kwilu and South Kivu provinces highlight the continued risk of sexual violence faced by women amid insecurity and armed activity, including attacks by both state forces and unidentified armed men. The incidents reflect recurring trends of sexual violence occurring in the context of everyday activities, armed attacks and insecurity along key transport routes.
