--- Report 2: OCHA (2026-09-22) ---
Somalia is bracing for widespread flooding as El-nino-induced heavy expected from October to December, with impacts potentially extending into 2027.
Flood-risk analysis projects significant El Niñoimpacts, with 3.4 million people at risk in 16 regions, particularly Hiraan, Middle Juba and Lower Juba.
Partners have stepped up preparedness, risk analysis, contingency planning, anticipatory action, community awareness and pre-positioning of supplies.
Displacement and eviction risks remain a major humanitarian concern, as conflict, climatic shocks and tenure insecurity heighten protection risks.
From January to June, 380 health facilities have either closed or operated at reduced capacity due to funding shortfalls; 115 mobile health units have closed.
