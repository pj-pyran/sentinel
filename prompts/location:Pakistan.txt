Crisis / situation: Pakistan

--- Report 1: Alkhidmat Foundation (2026-07-28) ---
HIGHLIGHTS
Alkhidmat Foundation Pakistan is closely monitoring the ongoing monsoon situation across the country, with particular focus on Azad Jammu &amp; Kashmir (AJK), Gilgit-Baltistan (GB), and Central Punjab. Heavy monsoon rains have triggered flash floods, riverine flooding, urban flooding, and Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOFs), affecting vulnerable communities and damaging homes, roads, bridges, and other critical infrastructure in several areas.
Alkhidmat's Disaster Management teams, volunteers, and response centers remain on high alert, coordinating with relevant authorities while continuously monitoring weather forecasts and river conditions. Rescue resources and emergency relief stocks have been pre-positioned to ensure a swift and effective response. Alkhidmat Foundation Pakistan remains committed to delivering timely rescue, relief, and humanitarian assistance to affected families throughout the ongoing monsoon emergency.

--- Report 2: WFP (2026-07-27) ---
HIGHLIGHTS JULY

Month-on-month, the Consumer Price Index (CPI) decreased by 0.3 percent in June compared to the previous month. The annual inflation increased by 11.07 percent (YoY) compared to June 2025, driven mainly by higher transport, housing, water, and energy costs. It is marginally lower compared to May 2026 CPI which stood at 11.7 percent.
Month-on-month food inflation increased by 0.96 percent mainly driven by increased costs of perishable food Items, while annual food inflation reached 9.4 percent compared to June 2025, driven by higher cost of wheat and wheat flour, cooking oil and perishable food items, meaning households paid 9 percent more for food - with low-income groups hit the hardest.
Prices of wheat and wheat flour increased in June compared to May, with wheat rising by 4.0 percent and wheat flour by 3.0 percent,signalling growing pressure on key staples. In contrast, rice prices remained largely stable with only a slight increase for rice Irri-6.
Petrol prices decreased month-on-month in June 2026, mainly due to lower global oil prices and domestic adjustments; however, year-on-year, petrol and diesel prices remained significantly higher by 15.9 percent and 18.6 percent, respectively.
Pakistan received USD 3.5 billion in remittances in June 2026, up 2 percent year-on-year but down 18.3 percent from the pre-Eid peak in May 2026.
