Crisis / situation: Pakistan

--- Report 1: WFP (2026-08-27) ---
HIGHLIGHTS JULY

Month-on-month, the Consumer Price Index (CPI) increased by 1.2 percent in July compared to the previous month. The annual inflation increased by 9.2 percent (YoY) compared to July 2025, driven mainly by higher transport, communication, and food costs. It is lower compared to June 2026 CPI (11.07 percent).
Month-on-month food inflation increased by 4.16 percent mainly driven by increased costs of perishable food Items, while annual food inflation reached 10.64 percent compared to July 2025, driven by higher cost of wheat and wheat flour, cooking oil and perishable food items, meaning households paid nearly 11 percent more for food - with low-income groups hit the hardest.
Prices of wheat and wheat flour increased in July compared to June, with wheat rising by 7.0 percent and wheat flour by 5.0 percent,signalling growing pressure on key staples. Price of wheat flour was highest in KP and lowest in Punjab. Rice prices remained stable with a slight increase by 2 percent for both rice varieties.
Petrol and Diesel prices recorded major increases in July 2026 compared to the previous month.Whereas on YoY basis prices were significantly higher compared to July 2025 prices with petrol 24 percent higher and diesel 38 percent higher, reflecting the impact of higher international oil prices and domestic fuel price revisions**.**
The average ToT was recorded at 11 kg of wheat flour which decreased by 5 percent MoM, while on YoY comparison it decreased (worsened) by 38 percent.

--- Report 4: Alkhidmat Foundation (2026-08-25) ---
HIGHLIGHTS
As ongoing monsoon rains continue to affect multiple regions across Pakistan during the 2026 monsoon season, Alkhidmat Foundation Pakistan has actively mobilized its network of Disaster Management teams and volunteers to extend critical support to impacted communities. The severe weather marked by heavy rainfall, localized flash floods, urban inundation, landslides, and Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOFs) has created substantial challenges for vulnerable populations, particularly across Gilgit-Baltistan (GB), Azad Jammu &amp; Kashmir (AJK), Central Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Central Punjab, and Balochistan.
Tragically, these weather events have resulted in 165 casualties and 496 injuries, while causing widespread destruction to private property and essential public infrastructure. A total of 1,567 homes have been either partially or fully damaged, leaving numerous families displaced and in urgent need of assistance.
In response to this human hardship, Alkhidmat Foundation remains fully committed to standing alongside affected families throughout the crisis. Dedicated relief workers and volunteers are on the ground delivering comprehensive humanitarian aid, including life-saving rescue support, temporary shelter, clean drinking water, hot meals, and mobile medical care.
