--- Report 1: WFP (2026-04-20) ---
KEY HIGHLIGHTS • WFP and the Punjab Social Protection Authority signed an agreement to strengthen climate-resilient, adaptive social protection by building evidence for climate-responsive models, enhancing rapid response systems, and integrating disaster-risk financing into social protection policies and programmes. • Through the Benazir Nashonuma Programme, WFP has supported over 4.4 million women and children nationwide since 2020, including 391,000 reached in March. • WFP Pakistan has published its 2025 Annual Country Report.

--- Report 2: WFP (2026-04-20) ---
- A comparative analysis of national average prices for key food items between 26 February (pre-Middle East conflict) and week ending 16th April, indicates a mixed trend with no change in price for staple commodities, but with increases in fresh items such as vegetables, and sharp increase in fuel prices, which has potential implications for households due to increased travel costs to markets. - Overall price stability was observed across staple foods, such as an 8% decrease in wheat flour prices driven by improved market availability while cooking oil and gram pulses decreased slightly, reflecting balanced domestic supply‑demand conditions. - The cost of a monthly basic food basket (wheat, pulses and oil) was PKR 14,346 per month or PKR 3,587 per week across the country.
