--- Report 1: IOM (2026-09-09) ---
Highlights

Cumulatively, from 15 September 2023 to 15 August 2026, 2,682,993 individuals have returned.

• During the reporting period, the number of returns (-43%) and deportations (-41%) decreased significantly compared to the previous reporting period (16–31 July 2026). The decrease in returns and deportations is likely linked to reduced arrest and enforcement operations during this reporting period.
• Since 1 April 2025, 1,828,960 Afghan nationals returned, of which 227,667 (12%) were deported.
• Fear of arrest (96%) remained the most important reason among respondents to return to Afghanistan.

--- Report 5: Alkhidmat Foundation (2026-09-07) ---
HIGHLIGHTS
The 2026 monsoon season has resulted in a significant humanitarian need across several regions of Pakistan. Heavy rainfall has triggered flash floods, urban flooding, landslides, and Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOFs), affecting communities in Gilgit-Baltistan (GB), Azad Jammu &amp; Kashmir (AJK), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP), the Federal Capital, Central and South Punjab, and Balochistan. As of the reporting period, 180 people have died, 509 have been injured, and 1,716 houses have been partially or fully damaged, further increasing humanitarian needs among affected populations. Alkhidmat Foundation Pakistan (AKFP) has mobilized its Disaster Management teams and volunteers to respond to the emerging needs. Response activities include emergency rescue operations, temporary shelter, safe drinking water, cooked meals, and mobile medical assistance. Teams continue to conduct field assessments and provide humanitarian support to affected communities.
