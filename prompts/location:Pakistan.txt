KEY HIGHLIGHTS
• Through the Government-led Benazir Nashonuma Programme, WFP has supported over 4.8 million pregnant and breastfeeding women and children under two since 2020, including 432,000 beneficiaries who received specialized nutritious food in July.
• Amid the ongoing monsoon season and increasing concerns over a potential strong El Niño event, WFP has strengthened preparedness measures through pre-positioned food stocks and enhanced cash-based transfer readiness.
• WFP and the Government of Balochistan provided 232,000 school meals in July, reaching 21,500 students across 49 public schools in Quetta.
