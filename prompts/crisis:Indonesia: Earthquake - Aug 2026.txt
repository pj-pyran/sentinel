Crisis / situation: Indonesia: Earthquake - Aug 2026

--- Report 1: MPBI (2026-08-19) ---
Situation Report
• BMKG noted that on Monday (August 17) there had been 1,576 aftershocks. BMKG appeals to people on Flores Island and its surroundings to remain calm, evacuate independently away from the beach area to the gathering point.
• Damage occurred to 264 educational facilities, 83 health facilities, 1 warehouse of the Logictics Affairs Agency (BULOG), 116 public facilities, 1 market, 157 houses of worship, 171 office units, 7 irrigation facilities, 34 road points, and 5 bridges.
• The Ministry of Health reported on August 17 at 07:30 WIB of the impact on 21 hospitals and 190 health centers. Most hospitals and health centers are still operating even though many services are carried out outside the building.
• The Coordination Meeting of the Reproductive Health Subcluster (Kespro) on August 17, 2026 in Flores, NTT, discussed the implementation of the Minimum Initial Service Package (PPAM) of Kespro as part of the earthquake emergency response. This package includes nine main components, ranging from the appointment of coordinators, prevention of sexual violence, handling HIV and STIs, to efforts to reduce maternal and neonatal mortality rates. In addition, PPAM also highlights the prevention of unwanted pregnancies, adolescent health, minimum services for toddlers and the elderly, and the integration of reproductive health services into basic services.

--- Report 2: IFRC (2026-08-18) ---
Sources for data marked as Other
PMI Assessment Team, BNPB
Description
A magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck offshore Nagekeo Regency, East Nusa Tenggara Province, on 15 August 2026 at 04:58 WIB. The epicentre was located approximately 30 kilometres northeast of Mbay at a depth of 15 kilometres. The earthquake triggered a tsunami warning, which was lifted at 07:31 WIB after sea-level changes were detected in several coastal areas.
The earthquake affected seven regencies in East Nusa Tenggara: Manggarai, East Manggarai, West Manggarai, Sikka, Ende, Nagekeo and Ngada. Impacts and displacement were also reported in West Nusa Tenggara and South Sulawesi.
As of 18 August 2026, 69 people were reported dead and 1,199 injured, including 115 people with serious injuries and 1,084 with minor injuries. A total of 46,252 people remain displaced, including 46,181 people across the seven affected regencies in East Nusa Tenggara and 71 people in Bima Regency, West Nusa Tenggara. Assessment and verification remain ongoing.
Preliminary damage includes 5,713 houses severely damaged, 2,098 moderately damaged and 3,402 slightly damaged. A further 283 educational facilities, 211 health facilities, 97 government buildings, 96 places of worship and 106 business premises or offices were affected.
As of 18 August 2026 at 05:00 WIB, BMKG had recorded 2,119 aftershocks ranging from magnitude 1.3 to 6.2. The Larantuka-Maumere road is accessible, while the Ende-Nagekeo road remains cut off by landslides and rock debris. Damaged roads, electricity outages and communication disruptions continue to affect assessments and humanitarian operations.

--- Report 3: MPBI (2026-08-18) ---
Situation Report
• Telecommunications services have been restored to 86.02 per cent. According to monitoring by the Ministry of Communications and Digital Affairs as of 16 August at 18:00 WIB, 683 of 794 base transceiver station (BTS) sites across 11 regencies/cities were operational; 111 sites remained out of service. Disruptions included power outages affecting 701 sites and transmission problems affecting 93 sites. Services in Alor, Lembata, and North Central Timor have been fully restored.
• Manggarai Regional Hospital sustained damage that affected clinical and administrative services. Patients are being treated temporarily in the hospital grounds. BNPB and the Ministry of Health will establish a field hospital at Golo Dukal Stadium in Ruteng. The facility is still being prepared and has not yet been declared operational.
• MDMC reported that many residents have chosen to remain outside their homes or in open areas because of trauma and concern about continuing aftershocks. Some displaced people are staying in public facilities, including sports halls and open fields, while others have erected emergency tents in the yards around their homes.
• The Larantuka–Maumere road is open and passable by logistics vehicles. The Ende– Nagekeo road remains completely cut off by landslide debris, and work to clear alternative routes is ongoing. Sections of the Ruteng–Km 210 and Aegela–Ende City roads have been affected by severe landslides. The Government is prioritising the deployment of heavy equipment to these areas.

--- Report 4: Human Initiative (2026-08-17) ---
EARTHQUAKE INFORMATION
Time of Incident: Saturday, 15 August 2026, at 04:58:24 WIB (05:58:24 WITA).
Magnitude: M 7.7 (BMKG / USGS). The earthquake was triggered by an upthrust fault mechanism (Flores Back Arc Thrust) at a shallow depth of 10–15 km. USGS estimates that up to 1.5 million people experienced strong shaking at intensity IV–V MMI across Flores Island to Sumbawa.
Tsunami Potential &amp; Detection: A tsunami early warning was temporarily activated by BMKG after sea-level rises were detected at 10 observation stations. The highest rise was recorded in Maurole, Ende, at 0.94 metres at 05:27 WIB. The tsunami early warning was officially lifted by BMKG at 07:30 WIB.
Aftershocks: BMKG recorded at least 54 aftershocks up to 07:30 WIB, with the strongest reaching M 6.1.
Earthquake Epicentre: Offshore at coordinates 8.41° S – 121.39° E, approximately 30 km northeast of Mbay, Nagekeo Regency, East Nusa Tenggara.

--- Report 6: IFRC (2026-08-17) ---
Description
An earthquake with a magnitude of 7.7 struck the Nagekeo Regency, East Nusa Tenggara (NTT) Province, on Saturday (15/08) at 04:58 WIB. The earthquake occurred at a depth of 15 km, with coordinates located at 8.41 S – 121.39 E, approximately 30 km Northeast of Mbay, Nagekeo Regency.
The earthquake triggered a tsunami warning. According to BMKG (Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency) monitoring, tsunami waves were detected in several areas with sea level heights reaching 0.94 meters in Maurole–Ende, 0.36 meters in Labuan Bajo, 0.38 meters at Kewapante Port–Sikka, and 0.25 meters in East Flores. The tsunami warning was officially lifted at 07:30 WIB.
Casualties and Damages by Region:

East Manggarai Regency: 14 fatalities and 2 people seriously injured. Data on building and public facility damage is currently being processed.
Manggarai Regency: 14 fatalities, 7 people seriously injured, 5 slightly injured, and 15 people displaced. Recorded damage includes 28 heavily damaged houses, 9 moderately damaged houses, and 9 slightly damaged houses.
Ende Regency: 2 fatalities and 1 person seriously injured. Impacted infrastructure includes 15 slightly damaged houses, 1 community health center (Puskesmas), and 1 house of worship.
Sikka Regency: 3 fatalities, 1 person seriously injured, 3 slightly injured, and 4 people reported missing.
Nagekeo, West Manggarai, and Ngada Regencies: These areas were affected; however, casualty and damage assessments are still ongoing.
Bima City: 1 person slightly injured, 2 moderately damaged houses, and 1 house of worship affected.

Total Impact Summary:
A total of 33 people have died, 11 are seriously injured, 11 have minor injuries, and 4 people remain missing. Furthermore, 15 individuals have been displaced. Infrastructure damage consists of 28 heavily damaged houses, 11 moderately damaged, 24 slightly damaged, 12 community health center, and 2 houses of worship.
Based on preliminary information from BNPB (National Disaster Management Authority), reports of casualties and damage to homes, government facilities, and infrastructure continue to come in from several affected areas. In Nagekeo Regency, approximately 2,000 residents conducted self-evacuations as a precaution following the quake and tsunami warning. Field verification and data collection are ongoing.
As of Saturday, August 15, 2026, at 02:55 pm, 172 aftershocks have been recorded with magnitudes ranging from 2.5 to 6.2. BMKG urges the public to remain vigilant regarding potential aftershocks. According to current reports, community activities in the affected areas have returned to normal. Meanwhile, rescue teams are still searching for 4 victims who reportedly fell from a boat during the event.

--- Report 7: IFRC (2026-08-17) ---
Description
A magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck offshore Nagekeo Regency, East Nusa Tenggara Province, on 15 August 2026 at 04:58 WIB. The earthquake occurred at a depth of 15 km, approximately 38 km northeast of Mbay. The tsunami warning issued for several coastal areas was officially lifted by BMKG at 07:31 WIB.
As of 16 August 2026 at 00:00 WIB, BNPB reported 47 fatalities and 101 injuries. More than 5,000 people remain displaced, including 3,356 people in Sikka, 2,078 in Manggarai, and 500 in Nagekeo. Additional displacement was reported in Bima and the Selayar Islands.
Preliminary assessments recorded 914 houses severely damaged, 126 moderately damaged, and 317 slightly damaged. At least 93 educational facilities, 36 health facilities, and 38 government offices were also affected.
BMKG recorded 341 aftershocks with magnitudes ranging from 2.5 to 6.2. Continuing aftershocks, damaged roads, electricity outages, and communication disruptions are affecting assessments and humanitarian operations. The Larantuka–Maumere road is accessible, while the Ende–Nagekeo road remains cut off. Data collection and verification are ongoing.

--- Report 8: MPBI (2026-08-17) ---
The NTT Provincial Government has set an emergency response status for 14 days, effective August 15-28, 2026 based on the Decree of the Governor of NTT Number 305/KEP/HK/2026. This status is the basis for resource mobilization and APBD financing for search, health services, IDPs, basic needs, and access and infrastructure recovery. Three districts have also imposed an emergency response period, namely in Manggara, Ngada, and East Manggarai.
The number of IDPs increased to 9,290 people, based on the BNPB update at 00.00 WIB. The details include 3,356 Sikka people—1,356 in the Samador GOR and 2,000 spread independently in 10 points—Manggarai 2,078 people, and Nagekeo 500 people. The rest are in other regions including Bima City and the Selayar Islands, but details have not been made public
The earthquake shock was followed by 341 aftershock activities. The number of fatalities recorded to date is 47 people, with the highest death toll in Manggarai Regency with 23 people and East Manggarai with 17 people. Meanwhile, other fatalities were spread across Sikka Regency with 4 people, West Manggarai with 1 person, Ende with 1 person, and Nagekeo with 1 person. At least 101 residents were reported injured with varying degrees of severity.
Damage increased significantly compared to previous reports: BNPB now records 914 houses severely damaged, 126 moderately damaged, and 317 lightly damaged—a total of 1,357 houses. The affected facilities include 93 educational facilities, 36 health facilities, and 38 government offices. This data is a temporary update at 00.00 WIB
Harian Antara reported that national road access was disrupted by landslides on the Malwatar – Ruteng City border, Ruteng-Km 210, Ruteng-Reo-Kedindi, Km 210 – Manggarai Regency boundary, Gako-Aigela – Ende City border, Danga-Maropokot, Ende-Wolowaru City, and Wolowaru-Maumere. In addition, five of the 15 airports in NTT were reported to be affected, but the entire runway was still usable; The damage is said to be limited to the terminal. Two ports were severely damaged and two crossing facilities were also affected. The location of each facility has not been detailed.
The IHCP network reported that several roads were cut off in Watungene, Komba City district, Maumere.
Land access to Reo, which was previously covered by landslides, has been able to be passed by SAR officers since 06.00 WITA. The second day of search operations was prioritized in Manggarai and East Manggarai, using land and sea routes.
