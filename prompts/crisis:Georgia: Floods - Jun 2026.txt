Crisis / situation: Georgia: Floods - Jun 2026

--- Report 1: Georgia Red Cross (2026-07-14) ---
Description
During the early hours of 12 July 2026, exceptionally heavy rainfall triggered flash floods and mudflows in Telavi Municipality, Kakheti Region. The Telaviskhevi River overflowed its banks after intense precipitation, causing widespread flooding across urban areas, significant damage to public infrastructure, residential buildings, businesses, and agricultural land. The event also generated large volumes of mud, rocks, and woody debris that overwhelmed sections of the river channel.

--- Report 2: Georgia Red Cross (2026-07-13) ---
Description
Strong hail and wind damaged the villages of the municipality. The situation was particularly difficult in the village of Gamarjveba, the roofs of houses, power lines were damaged. There was heavy rain in all the villages of the municipality, but the village of Gamarjveba was particularly damaged, where the disaster toppled trees and damaged part of the power lines. The roads were closed, but the response was prompt, both from the municipal services, as well as the fire and rescue service.
The heavy rain also created problems in the Akhmeta municipality. As a result of heavy rainfall, the drainage channels were unable to handle a large flow of water, which flooded the yards, basements and first floors of several residential buildings in the village of Matani.
