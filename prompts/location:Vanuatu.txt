Crisis / situation: Vanuatu

--- Report 1: Govt. Vanuatu (2026-09-02) ---
Key highlights:

Rotavirus has been laboratory confirmed as the cause of the acute watery diarrhoea outbreak on Tanna Island.
A cumulative 260 cases have been reported between EW30 and EW35, with 153 cases reported in EW35 alone, representing the highest weekly case count recorded to date.
Transmission remains ongoing across Tanna Island, with cases reported from all surveillance sentinel sites.
Children under five years account for 87% of reported cases, with children under two years most affected.
One death has been reported in a one-year-old child with severe dehydration and underlying malnutrition.
Response activities continue, including enhanced surveillance, case management, health promotion, and community engagement.

--- Report 2: Govt. Vanuatu (2026-08-31) ---
Overview

From 10 August to 25 August 2026, a total of 81 cases of watery diarrhoea were reported through Lenakel Hospital.
The weekly number of reported cases exceeded the outbreak alert threshold of 34 cases, resulting in the declaration of a watery diarrhoea outbreak in Tanna Island.
Most cases were reported through Lenakel Hospital.
Hotspot communities include Lounuloul, Imai, Isla, Lamtavekel, Loueiau, and lmarap.
Case definition: Three or more loose or watery stools within a 24-hour period.

Key highlights:

81 cases reported between 10 and 25 August 2026.
Cases exceeded the outbreak alert threshold, resulting in the declaration of an outbreak.
Most cases are among children under 5 years of age.
Seven cases have been hospitalised, with one severe case reported and no deaths to date.
Stool samples have been collected and are awaiting laboratory results to identify the causative agent.
Community awareness, enhanced surveillance, and case management activities are ongoing.
