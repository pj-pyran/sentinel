Crisis / situation: Vanuatu

--- Report 1: Govt. Vanuatu (2026-06-03) ---
Key Highlights:

16 new cases of ciguatera fish poisoning (CFP) reported between EpiWeek 19–21, bringing the cumulative total to 188 cases.
Cases have significantly declined, but remained above the alert threshold in EpiWeek 19 and 21; only 3 cases were reported in EpiWeek 20.
Males account for 61% of cases, while females account for 39%.
Highest number of cases among individuals aged 15–34 years; fewer cases in those 35+, and lowest in children ≤14 years.
Majority of cases linked to unspecified “other” fish (103 cases) and reef fish (71 cases), with smaller numbers linked to other fish types.
Cases reported across 10 islands, with Efate accounting for 47% of reported cases.
The Surveillance Team will continue close monitoring and provide updates as needed

--- Report 2: Govt. Vanuatu (2026-06-03) ---
Key highlights:

From EW20–EW22, 2 new cases were reported, bringing the total to 10, with no hospitalizations or deaths.
Most cases (80%) were from Pango, with 70% female and 60% aged 26+. Some cases reported recent travel to Fiji, suggesting epidemiological links.
No outbreak has been declared. Case numbers remain below the alert threshold, and monitoring continues.

--- Report 3: SPC (2026-06-03) ---
Key Highlights:

From EW19–EW21, 7 RSV cases and 1 influenza case were reported in EW21, bringing totals to 33 RSV and 9 influenza cases in 2026. ILI activity increased (489, 478, 565) but remained below the alert threshold, while SARI decreased (10, 9, 8).
Most cases (84%) were in children aged 0–4 years. RSV accounted for the largest burden, and cases were slightly higher among females overall, though males accounted for most meningitis and influenza cases.
Efate reported the majority of cases, including all RSV, influenza, and SARS-CoV-2 cases. Pentecost and Tanna each reported one meningitis case.
No outbreak has been declared; however, respiratory virus circulation continues to be closely monitored
