Crisis / situation: Ethiopia

--- Report 1: UNHCR (2026-05-25) ---
Highlights
UNHCR and partners strengthened protection delivery through coordinated relocation efforts, ensuring safer living conditions and improved access to essential services for refugees in Luakdong. Community-based protection interventions remained active, with child-friendly spaces and awareness campaigns providing psychosocial support and promoting protection from gender-based violence and harmful practices. Engagement with refugee communities, including children and adolescents, enhanced participation in protection activities and social cohesion. Ongoing border monitoring and protection assessments provided insights into the needs and risks faced by newly arriving populations, informing timely and targeted responses across the operational areas.

--- Report 2: UNHCR (2026-05-25) ---
Highlights
Health partners sustained the delivery of essential primary healthcare services across Luakdong, Tormorok, and Muon, ensuring continued access to consultations, maternal care, and emergency services for displaced and host populations. Expanded facility-based care and the introduction of continuous service provision improved management of critical cases and reduced pressure on referrals. Preventive efforts were reinforced through vaccination campaigns and community-based health education, strengthening awareness and early health-seeking behavior. Infrastructure improvements, including the rehabilitation of damaged health facilities, supported uninterrupted service delivery, while coordinated outreach activities enhanced engagement with affected communities.

--- Report 4: UNHCR (2026-05-25) ---
Highlights
Site development and shelter interventions showed moderate improvement progress, with continued but gradual progress in road works, drainage, and culvert installations aimed at enhancing site accessibility and resilience. The expansion of lighting infrastructure contributed to improved safety within the settlement. Shelter construction moved forward steadily, though not at full capacity, with most upgradable shelters completed and ongoing efforts supporting remaining households through material distribution and site allocation. Infrastructure development, including administrative buildings, schools, and water facilities, progressed at a moderate pace, supporting service delivery. Coordination among partners and ongoing site assessments supported planning for further expansion.

--- Report 5: UNHCR (2026-05-25) ---
Highlights
UNHCR and partners continued to deliver critical WASH services across Luakdong and Tormorok, ensuring sustained access to safe water despite operational constraints. Water production, treatment, and distribution systems remained functional, supported by ongoing maintenance and water-quality monitoring to ensure safe consumption. Network expansion works progressed, including pipeline installation, trenching, and borehole development, aimed at improving access to underserved areas. In parallel, sanitation interventions advanced through the construction of household and communal facilities, alongside bathing shelters and slab production to support community-led latrine construction. Hygiene-promotion activities were intensified through community outreach, environmental cleaning campaigns, and awareness sessions, strengthening safe water handling, sanitation practices, and community ownership of WASH facilities.
