Crisis / situation: Central African Republic

--- Report 1: OCHA (2026-08-21) ---
HIGHLIGHTS

In Zémio, insecurity is driving population movements towards Zapaï in DRC.
More than 4,000 people affected by torrential rains are receiving cash assistance in Bangassou.
In Am-Dafock, access to safe drinking water has improved for more than 11,500 internally displaced persons.

GENERAL CONTEXT
Haut-Mbomou Prefecture, South-East

From 11 to 14 August, population movements were reported from Komboni, located about 5 km from Zémio, to Zapaï in the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC). The movements followed military operations conducted against armed elements in the area. Persistent insecurity continues to affect livelihoods by restricting access to farmland and disrupting harvesting activities. As food security conditions deteriorate, some households have been forced to sell part of the essential household items received through humanitarian assistance to meet their immediate food needs.

HUMANITARIAN NEEDS AND RESPONSE
Multi-sector
Ouham-Fafa Prefecture, North-West

From 15 to 20 August, COOPADEM strengthened the livelihoods of 45 members of local associations from both host communities and displaced populations at the Gbazara site in Batangafo. Beneficiaries received a community micro-grant and training on management and sustainable monitoring of income-generating activities to enhance their economic self-reliance. The initiative also aims to strengthen beneficiaries’ participation in youth-led community mobilization and awareness-raising activities, particularly on social cohesion and protection of internally displaced persons (IDPs), thereby contributing to more inclusive governance of displacement sites and host communities. In parallel, soap-making activities enabled women to develop livelihood opportunities while improving the living conditions of vulnerable households. These activities were implemented with financial support from the Central African Republic Humanitarian Fund (CAR HF).

Food Security
Vakaga Prefecture, North-East

From 11 to 14 August, Triangle Génération Humanitaire (TGH) provided cash assistance to 22 agricultural groups in Birao. The intervention benefited 1,000 households from host communities and returnee populations. Beneficiaries also received training on fodder crop production techniques to strengthen their livelihoods and enhance their agricultural production capacity.

--- Report 2: UNHCR (2026-08-19) ---
UNHCR's operations in the Central African Republic (CAR) are based on protection through presence, a central pillar of the humanitarian response. In a context marked by security volatility and the repercussions of the Sudanese crisis, particular attention is also being paid to emergency response. At the same time, the search for durable solutions is a major priority, by supporting the voluntary, safe and dignified return of Central African refugees, while ensuring their sustainable reintegration and their contribution to national development. The Central African Government, UNHCR and partners are providing protection and multi-sectoral assistance to internally displaced persons (IDPs) and refugees, including Chadians and Sudanese, while promoting durable solutions for Central African returnees. UNHCR aligns its interventions with national priorities, including the National Development Programme, the National Strategy for Durable Solutions and the Platform to Support Solutions from the 2022 Yaoundé Declaration.
