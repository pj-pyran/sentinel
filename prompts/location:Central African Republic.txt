Crisis / situation: Central African Republic

--- Report 1: OCHA (2026-11-20) ---
HIGHLIGHTS

Despite a still volatile context, some humanitarian movements have resumed around Zémio.
Infrastructure has been installed in Ouham-Pendé to help prevent agro-pastoral conflicts and reduce tensions between herders and farmers.
3,900 students received school kits in Kaga-Bandoro and M’brès.

GENERAL CONTEXT
Haut-Mbomou Prefecture – South-East
The security situation remains volatile in the Zémio area, despite a relative improvement recently observed along certain roads around the town. In this context, humanitarian actors have gradually resumed some movements and activities in areas affected by movement restrictions that have been in place since September. A limited resumption of civilian movement has also been observed on the main road corridors, while staff from peripheral health facilities have been able to travel to Zémio to replenish medical supplies. No incidents have recently been reported on these routes. A humanitarian organization also conducted movements along the Zémio-Dembia axis to carry out assessments. Given the continuing volatility of the security environment, humanitarian partners are closely monitoring developments and maintaining existing security and risk-management measures.
HUMANITARIAN NEEDS AND RESPONSE
Health
Haute-Kotto Prefecture – Central
On 11 November, the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA) and its partner International Medical Corps (IMC) concluded their campaign providing comprehensive care for women living with obstetric fistula. A total of 33 patients received specialized treatment in Bria, with an 88 per cent success rate. 15 other patients faced transportation challenges in reaching Bria from Ndele in Bamingui-Bangoran Prefecture. To improve access to services in that area, a second two-week campaign is being conducted in Ndele from 12 to 26 November 2026.
Multi-sector
Vakaga Prefecture – North-East
On 11 November, the NGO COHEB, under a labour-intensive work project implemented through the Emergency Project to Support the Stabilization and Recovery of Refugees and Host Communities in the Lake Chad Basin (PASRRC), distributed cash assistance to 400 beneficiaries, including 150 Sudanese refugees and 250 members of host communities. Through the cash-for-work approach, each beneficiary received 30,000 CFA francs to support livelihoods and meet priority household needs.

--- Report 2: UNHCR (2026-11-20) ---
UNHCR's operations in the Central African Republic (CAR) are built on protection through presence, the cornerstone of the humanitarian response. Amid volatile security conditions and the spillover of the crisis in Sudan, UNHCR also remains ready to respond to emergencies. Durable solutions are another top priority supporting the voluntary, safe, and dignified return of Central African refugees, their sustainable reintegration, and their contribution to national development. Together with the Government of CAR and its partners, UNHCR provides protection and multisectoral assistance to internally displaced people (IDPs) and refugees, including Sudanese and Chadian refugees, while promoting durable solutions for Central Africans returning home. Its work is aligned with national priorities: the National Development Plan, the National Strategy for Durable Solutions and the Solutions Support Platform established under the 2022 Yaoundé Declaration.
