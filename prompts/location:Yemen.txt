Crisis / situation: Yemen

--- Report 1: FAO (2026-04-21) ---
KEY HIGHLIGHTS SHORT-TERM OUTLOOK AND IMPLICATIONS ON FOOD SECURITY: The food security outlook for Yemen in early 2026 remains alarming, with 18.3 million people facing Crisis or worse levels of acute food insecurity (IPC Phase 3+) and the country bearing the world’s highest burden of populations in Phase 4. While localized exchange-rate stability in Government-controlled areas offers fragile relief, the situation in SBA-controlled areas is critical due to a full pause in UN operations and severe funding shortfalls, with the 2026 humanitarian response less than 10 percent funded as of March. --- Report 2: WFP (2026-04-21) ---
- Continued regional tensions are heightening food security risks in Yemen while causing supply chain disruptions that are jeopardizing WFP’s ability to stabilize food access. - All United Nations Humanitarian Air Service (UNHAS) flights to and from Yemen have been suspended until further notice due to a lack of the necessary clearances. - WFP continues to call for the release of its 38 staff members detained by the Sana’a-based authorities. All United Nations activities in northern Yemen remain suspended.
