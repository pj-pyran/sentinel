Crisis / situation: Yemen

--- Report 1: WFP (2026-08-25) ---
HIGHLIGHTS

The food security situation continues to deteriorate, with 36 percent of the population facing severe food deprivation.
As hostilities flare along the frontlines, WFP is maintaining a high level of operational preparedness and is actively reviewing its contingency arrangements.
The UN Special Envoy for Yemen warns that Yemen now faces a greater risk of renewed large-scale conflict than at any point since the UN-brokered truce of April 2022.
WFP started the fifth targeted emergency food assistance (TEFA) cycle in areas under the Government of Yemen in mid-August, targeting 1.7 million people.

--- Report 2: OCHA (2026-08-25) ---
Highlights
• 9 civilians reportedly killed and 54 injured due to reported escalation across Marib, Ta’iz and west coast areas.
• 162 households displaced across Ta’iz, Al Hodeidah, Lahj and Marib.
• Al Makha Port suspended operations; shelter damage reported in Marib.
• Pressure on health services affected referrals • Telecommunications disruptions and affected humanitarian operations.
• Humanitarian response is ongoing, gaps remain in protection, shelter, cash assistance and emergency preparedness.
