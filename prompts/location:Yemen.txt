Crisis / situation: Yemen

--- Report 1: UNHCR (2026-11-24) ---
Highlights
▪ Displacement in Yemen continues to rise, with more than 360,000 people newly displaced since 1 August, up from around 321,000 in the previous update. Taiz remains the most affected governorate, accounting for 64% of new displacement.
▪ Cross-border movements continue across the Gulf of Aden, with arrivals reaching 4,9191 individuals, including 4,439 arrivals in Djibouti and 480 arrivals in Somalia. During the reporting period, 115 new arrivals from Yemen were recorded in Somalia.
▪ Insecurity is driving further refugee movements within Yemen, with 4,638 refugees relocating from Kharaz camp to Aden since 7 November. An assisted spontaneous return flight to Somalia for more than 100 refugees has been postponed to 28 December.

--- Report 2: IOM (2026-11-24) ---
IOM Yemen DTM’s Rapid Displacement Tracking (RDT) tool collects data on estimated numbers of households forced to flee on a daily basis from their locations of origin or displacement, allowing for regular reporting of new displacements in terms of estimated numbers, geography, and needs. It also tracks returnees who returned to their location of origin.
From 1 January to 21 November 2026, IOM Yemen DTM tracked 65,427 households (HH) (392,562 Individuals) who experienced displacement at least once.
Between 15 and 21 November 2026, IOM Yemen DTM tracked 2622 households (15732 individuals) displaced at least once. The majority of people moved into/within the following governorates and districts:

Lahj (1589 HHs) – Tuban (1057 HHs), Tur Al Bahah (302 HHs), Al Madaribah Wa Al Aarah (90 HHs) districts. Most displacements in the governorate were internal.
Ta’iz (401 HHs) – Sabir Al Mawadim (71 HHs), Jabal Habashi (70 HHs), Ash Shamayatayn (67 HHs) districts. Most displacements in the governorate were internal.
Aden (382 HHs) – Al Burayqah (295 HHs), Dar Sad (84 HHs), Kritar - Sirah (3 HHs) districts. Most displacements in the governorate originated from Lahj.

The majority of people moved from the following governorates and districts:

Lahj (1732 HHs) – Al Madaribah Wa Al Aarah (1139 HHs), Tur Al Bahah (459 HHs), Al Maqatirah (122 HHs) districts.
Ta’iz (750 HHs) – Al Misrakh (130 HHs), Jabal Habashi (122 HHs), Al Makha (93 HHs) districts.
Al Hodeidah (81 HHs) – Al Khukhah (45 HHs), Hays (28 HHs), Al Hawak (2 HHs) districts.

IOM identified 442 additional households displaced in the previous reporting period, which covered 08 - 14 November 2026, in the governorates of Aden (178 HHs), Abyan (98 HHs), Ma’rib (76 HHs), Ad Dali (41 HHs), Al Maharah (35 HHs), and Hadramawt (14 HHs). These figures have been added to the cumulative displacement total recorded since the beginning of the year.
