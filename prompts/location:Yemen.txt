Crisis / situation: Yemen

--- Report 1: IOM (2026-09-18) ---
IOM Yemen DTM continues to record the displacement caused by escalation in the west coast. An approximate of 109,356 individuals (approximately 18,226 households1) were recorded between 1 and 16 September, making the cumulative figure recorded since the escalation began in June 2026 reach 112,818 individuals (approximately 18,803 households).
The largest concentration of displaced people was reported in Ta’iz Governorate, hosting 64,314 individuals (57%), followed by Lahj Governorate, with 30,132 individuals (27%). Additional displacement was recorded in Al Hodeidah, with 8,244 individuals (7%); Aden, with 5,688 individuals (5%); Abyan, with 3,324 individuals (3%); Hadramawt, with 660 individuals (less than 1%); and Ma’rib, with 456 individuals (less than 1%).

--- Report 2: IMC (2026-09-17) ---
FAST FACTS

12,597 newly displaced households (86,415 individuals) have been reported across Abyan, Aden, Al Hodeidah, Lahj, Shabwah and Taiz governorates.
Fifteen of 19 International Medical Corps-supported health facilities remain operational across the west coast, with four non-functional.

OUR FOOTPRINT

International Medical Corps maintains a wide operational presence across Hajjah, Ibb and Sana’a governorates in the north, and Aden, Al Dhale’e, Al Hodeidah, Lahj and Taiz governorates in the south. Our lifesaving multi-sectoral assistance prioritizes active conflict zones and high-risk environments. In 2025, we reached more than 400,000 people with integrated humanitarian programming.

OUR RESPONSE

International Medical Corps continues to support a network of health facilities across the west coast in Al Khawkhah, Al Mokha, Al Mudhafar, Hays, Mawza and Salah districts.
Seven facilities resumed service delivery after temporary disruption, reflecting strong community engagement and health workforce commitment.
International Medical Corps is deploying an integrated mobile medical team in Lahj governorate to provide displaced populations with primary healthcare, maternal and newborn services, nutrition screening and treatment, mental health care, disease surveillance and referral services.
