--- Report 2: Health Cluster (2026-05-13) ---
A total of 1080 Health Facilities (186 Hospitals, 374 Health Centers , 503 Health Units, and 17 Others ) are being supported by Health Cluster Partners. - During the reporting period of November and December 2025, Health Cluster conducted a total of 7 Health Cluster coordination meetings and 12 ad hoc meetings at the National, AoR, and Hub levels (Aden, Al Hudaydah, Ibb/Taizz, Sa’dah, Sana’a, Marib, Hadramout, and West Coast). - Health Cluster conducted and attended over 37 technical meetings, which include but are not limited to ICCM, RCT, TWGs, inter-sectoral, and government meetings. The cluster also conducted 5 field visits and over 35 bilateral meetings with partners, donors, government entities, and stakeholders during the reporting period. Furthermore, the Health Cluster attended/ Conducted 3 trainings and 2 Taskforce meetings.
