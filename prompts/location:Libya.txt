Crisis / situation: Libya

--- Report 1: UNHCR (2026-08-12) ---
Overview
It is estimated that over 559,920 Sudanese refugees have arrived in Libya since April 2023. This includes 76,904 individuals who were registered post-conflict, bringing the total number of Sudanese registered by UNHCR at its registration centre in Tripoli to 93,159. Increased border monitoring by Libyan authorities has led refugees to take longer routes, and arrivals via Chad have decreased to an average of 70 per day. Due to the irregular nature of entry, the ad hoc data shared by authorities, and the vast remote land border with Chad, Egypt, and Sudan, combined with movements towards cities along the coast, it remains challenging to provide an accurate number of Sudanese refugees.

--- Report 3: UNHCR (2026-08-12) ---
Overview
It is estimated that over 566,043 Sudanese refugees have arrived in Libya since April 2023. This includes 78,079 individuals who were registered post-conflict, bringing the total number of Sudanese registered by UNHCR at its registration centre in Tripoli to 93,831. Increased border monitoring by Libyan authorities has led refugees to take longer routes, and arrivals via land borders have reached an average of 80 per day including entries from Egypt, Chad, and Niger. Due to the irregular nature of entry, the ad hoc data shared by authorities, and the vast remote land border with Chad, Egypt, and Sudan, combined with movements towards cities along the coast, it remains challenging to provide an accurate number of Sudanese refugees.
